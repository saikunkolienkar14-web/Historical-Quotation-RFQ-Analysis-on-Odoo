Dear Sir, 
 
Refer to your subject RFQ we are pleased to submit our proposal as under: 
The proposed CEMS and liquid analyser systems are designed for safe area installation. For any tags without 
specified measuring ranges, standard ranges have been assumed. Utilities such as instrument air, nitrogen, and 
cooling water shall be provided by the customer, along with all field cabling/tubing to stack, vent, and drain 
points outside the battery limit. Customer will also ensure connectivity to relevant authorities. MODBUS TCP/IP 
output will be available from the CEMS analyser panels. Tentative distances from process tapping points to analyser 
cabinets are considered as 50 m for each CEMS cabinet and 15 m for each extractive liquid analyser system. 
CEMS (Tags CEMS-01 & CEMS-02 – Hot Oil Heater & Boiler): 
• 
Systems are housed in IP54-rated cabinets, to be installed in an existing shelter or temperature-controlled room 
(5–45 °C). 
• 
Stack process details are pending; the customer is requested to provide the completed stack details sheet to 
confirm flowmeter and SHS compatibility. 
• 
Each panel includes temperature and pressure measurements and a combustible gas detector. 
Liquid Analyser Systems: 
• 
Boiler Feed Water (Tags 6400-AT-6403 pH, 6400-AT-6404 Conductivity, 6400-AT-6402 DO): Due to high 
temperature (~200 °C), an extractive sampling system with heat exchanger has been included, as per the 
P&ID. 
• 
Cooling Water Return (Tags 6500-AT-6501 pH, 6500-AT-6502 Conductivity): Due to high pressure (~8.5 bar), 
sensors will be installed in an extractive system with pressure regulation, per the P&ID. 
• 
Waste Water (Tag 6400-AT-6401 pH): Direct in-situ measurement is feasible and included.
