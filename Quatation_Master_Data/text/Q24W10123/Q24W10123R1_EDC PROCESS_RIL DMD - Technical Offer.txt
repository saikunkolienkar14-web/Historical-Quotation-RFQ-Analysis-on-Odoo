SECTION 2 : ASSUMPTIONS/CLARIFICATIONS 
 
1. Hazardous area classification is mentioned as Zone 2 llC T3 in ‘‘Sr no 4 of datasheet‘‘ for Analysers, 
SHS & Probe. However in ‘‘Sr no 10 of datasheet‘‘ hazardous area is mentioned as Zone 1 llC T3. 
We understand that Hazardous area to be considered for all analysers and other electrical 
equipments for this Process package as Zone 1 llC T3. 
 
2. Calibration gas cylinders/Buffer solutions shall be supplied for 6 months operation from the date of 
commissioning. Further refilling shall be taken care by customer. 
 
3. All field power and signal cables shall be in RIL scope of supply, However any special cables 
required between sensor unit & controller unit shall be considered by Adage. 
 
4. Supply of N2 @5-7 Kg/cm2 at stack for Oxygen analyser & Near the shelter shall be in RIL scope 
of supply. 
 
5. Supporting structure, Trays, clamps and other accessories for laying of all signal, power and 
communication cables and field tubing’s including EHT bundles shall be taken care by RIL. 
 
6. We shall provide Modbus serial output multidropped at shelter side using necessary converters at 
both ends i.e Shelter & MCR room. FO cable shall be in RIL scope of supply 
 
7. SHS and other accessories makes shall be as per RIL AVL. 
 
8. All Liquid analyser with EDC sample are unsafe to handle inside closed shelter hence quoted 
separately inside 3 sided shelter MOC: SS316 which shall consists of Moisture in EDC & Chlorine 
tags. 
 
9. Shelters construction & sampling system shall be as per RIL standard. 
 
10. For all ethylene measurement tags, sample is being converted to vapour at tapping itself and same 
shall be taken upto analyser using Heated tube and heated SHS system. 
 
11. On/Off valves for each tag to Automatically cutoff sample incase of emergency to be considered by 
Adage 
 
12. Fuel gas and carrier gas cylinders shall be in RIL scope of supply, however accessories like ACU, 
valves, gauges along with cylinder rack will be supplied by Adage
