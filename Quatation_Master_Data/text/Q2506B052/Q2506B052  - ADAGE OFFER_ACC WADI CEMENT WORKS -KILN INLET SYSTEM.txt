TYPICAL PROBLEMS OBSERVED IN A FEW KILN INLET INSTALLATION 

1. The Probe Filter need to be cleaned once every day. With the clogging of 
the filter the flow was reducing. 
 
2. There is a huge deposition of Materials on the probe which has to be 
poked and removed every two hours. 

3. The Oxygen reading was on the higher side- though there was no 
leakage in the system 
 
4. At the Entry point of the Gas in the probe (port of the probe) was getting 
blocked with dust and as a result flow was reducing. 

5. The Blow back is required every 10 to 15 minutes and reading is not 
available for 1 to 1.5 minutes

ADVANTAGES OF OPERATION & MAINTAINANCE SPECS 
 
a) 
Ultramat 23 Analyzer from SIEMENS have been extremely rugged and with improved design. The 
sensor is not exposed in the sample gas path and the sensor does not fail due to moisture or dust 
ingress. This is an extremely useful advantage as the sensor life in SIEMENS is much higher. Feedback 
can be taken on this from any SIEMENS analyser user. There are installations for more than 20 years 
where the sensor has not been replaced till date. 
 
b) The CO, NO,SO2 analysis is done through Ultramat 23 analyzer. The Ultramat 23 uses the advanced 
triple layer detector. 
 
Additionally the single beam technology in the Ultramat 23 Analyzer allows extremely low drift. The 
conventional dual beam technology used in the CONVENTIONAL models has disadvantages of higher 
influence due to Dirt in sample cell, aging of electronics, reduced IR intensity. Hence frequent calibrations 
with calibration cylinders are required. 
 
In the single beam technology the compensation is much easier;- 
Illustrated as U = Uo X e-ⁿª where the U is detector signal and Uo is base signal with zero gas and na 
is constant multiplied by the concentration of component to be measured . S is the signal generated as 
a ratio of the Uo and U. If F is the influencing factor due to which the detector calibration is required 
frequently , in single beam technology , only F.Uo to be measured frequently by calibration thru the 
non IR gas like ambient air . 
 
Thus till the next calibration cycle this value of F.Uo is used for calculating S, so that the measured value 
F.U gives correct value of S. 
 
Hence the mentioned influence by dirt or moisture or ageing will have only an insignificant effect to the 
measurement as long as the F.Uo is in certain tolerance limit which is monitored thru the Analyser 
electronics. The analyser thus uses ambient air with inbuilt pump for auto calibration. Only once in a year 
or in 18 months calibration is required with a certified cylinder. Therefore the analyzers are virtually 
maintenance free.
