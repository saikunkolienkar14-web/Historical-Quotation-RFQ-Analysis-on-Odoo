I. Maintenance of H2 purity Analyser & Systems shall include the following; 
 
 M/S Adage Engineer will check the following during priventive maintenance visits. 
 
1. Functioning of all system and sub-system of analyser, e.g. pumps, probes, filters, sensors, main

2. Flushing & cleaning of the sample lines, leak checks, temperature and suction levels etc. 
 
3. Preventive maintenance of Sample Handling System including filters, internal tubes etc. Back 
purging of the sample line will be done as recommended in schedule (Whichever is earlier) 
depending upon requirement. 
 
4. To monitor any corrosion and take suitable measures to prevent them. In order to avoid corrosion

5. Functioning of all the accessories connected with the system will be checked. 
 
6. Functional checking and calibration of the total system. 
 
7. Checking of the healthiness of other instruments such as PLC's, HMI’s & other electrical instruments 
connected with the system. 
 
8. Calibration of the analyser with the help of suitable standard gases of appropriate range and duly 
certified. 
 
9. Verify room environment and report to M/s NPCIL site in-charge for doing the needful, if needed.
