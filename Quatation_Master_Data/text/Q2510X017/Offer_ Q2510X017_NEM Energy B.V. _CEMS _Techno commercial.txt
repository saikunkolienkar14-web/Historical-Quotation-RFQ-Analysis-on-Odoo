FEATURES OF THE OFFERED SYSTEM 
 
• 
Advanced NOx Measurement Without Converters 
Our solution includes the Siemens Ultramat 23 analyzer, which measures NO and NO₂ independently within the same 
unit. This eliminates the need for any external NOx converter, delivering more reliable and simplified NOx calculation. 
• 
Built-in Protection Features 
The Ultramat 23 comes equipped with integrated sample gas flowmeters, a condensate trap, and dust filters, 
providing enhanced protection and longer analyzer life. 
• 
Dual Technology Advantage (NDIR + UV) 
The analyzer combines NDIR and UV principles: SO₂ and NO₂ are measured by UV, while NO is measured by NDIR. This 
unique combination enables highly sensitive measurement of low SO₂ concentrations meeting stringent regulatory 
requirements. 
• 
Robust Sample Gas Handling 
Given such critical application we offer a SS316 sample gas probe designed for durability and performance under 
demanding conditions. 
• 
Intelligent Moisture Protection 
A Condensate Monitor is integrated into the system. Upon detecting moisture, it interlocks with the analyzer and 
automatically cuts off the sample pump, preventing sampling and protecting the analyzer from potential damage. 
• 
Automated Cleaning System 
The analyzer probe features timer-based automatic blowback/cleaning, ensuring uninterrupted operation and minimal 
manual maintenance. 
• 
Heated Sampling Lines 
Sample gas tubes are heated throughout the transport path, eliminating cold spots and preventing sample 
condensation, thus preserving measurement accuracy. 
• 
User-Friendly Touchscreen Interface 
The system includes a 7” touchscreen display, providing real-time indication of sampling/purging conditions, fault 
alarms, and comprehensive fault logging for easy review and traceability.
