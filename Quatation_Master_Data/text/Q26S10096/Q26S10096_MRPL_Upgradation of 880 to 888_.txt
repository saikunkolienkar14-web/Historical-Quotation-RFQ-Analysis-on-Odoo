SECTION 2: TECHNICAL LITERATURE 
 
Why Upgrade to AMETEK 888 from 880? 
 
Ametek 880 
 
Ametek 888 

1. Increased Reliability – Improved thermal management extends electronics life and reduces 
unexpected failures. 
2. Predictive Diagnostics – Smart monitoring detects early warning signs, minimizing unplanned 
downtime. 
3. Better Process Protection – Automatic flow control and flange temperature alarms help prevent 
analyzer damage. 
4. Higher Ambient Capability – Operates in hotter environments without requiring additional cooling 
systems. 
5. Modern Connectivity – Ethernet, Modbus, and web-based interface allow remote monitoring and 
easier integration with DCS systems. 
6. Direct Replacement Design – Engineered for easier upgrade from existing 880-NSL installations 
with minimal modification.
