TECHNICAL SCOPE 

We shall carry out quartely priventive maintenance & one breakdown visit of online CEMS systems 
supplied against supply PO 9301000466 at GSFC Vadodara site. 

Maintenance of Gas Analyser & Systems shall include the following during the visit; 
 
 M/s Adage Engineer will check the following during Routine Maintenance visits. 
 
1. Functioning of all system and sub-system of analyser, e.g. pumps, probes, filters, sensors, main 
analyser, power supplies etc. 
 
2. Preventive maintenance of probes. 
 
3. Flushing & cleaning of the sample lines, leak checks, temperature and suction levels etc. 
 
4. Preventive maintenance of Sample Handling System including filters, internal tubes etc. Back 
purging of the filters will be done as per commands from PLC, or as recommended in schedule 
(Whichever is earlier) depending upon requirement. Once filter is choked or worn out same shall be 
replaced. 
 
5. To monitor any corrosion and take suitable measures to prevent them. In order to avoid corrosion 
to probes and other parts of the system, it would require routine inspection. 
 
6. Checking Healthiness of heated probe, Condensate Monitors, Sample gas coolers and Other 
components. 
 
7. Functioning of all the accessories connected with the system will be checked. 
 
8. Functional checking and calibration of the total system. 
9. The systems shall be operated and maintained on regular basis and monitoring of data which is 
interlinked with CPCB I SPCB Server I Website on continuous basis by ISGEC. 
 
10. Regular Functional Checking and calibration of the Dust Monitors to ensure its accuracy with the 
help of the isokinetic result, isokinetic sampling is ISGEC scope. 
 
11. Checking of the Data Connectivity & CEMS PC by remote support.
