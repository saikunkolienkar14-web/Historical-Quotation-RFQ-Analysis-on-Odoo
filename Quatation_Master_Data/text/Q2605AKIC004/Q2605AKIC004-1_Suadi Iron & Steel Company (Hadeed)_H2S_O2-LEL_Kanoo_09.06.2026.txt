General Notes: 
 
1. Based on the process conditions and parameters provided, we have assumed that the process/feed gas is free 
of moisture and dust. However, as stated in our quotation, the process gas must be moisture-free to ensure 
accurate H₂S measurement. 
 
There are several factors that need to be considered with respect to H₂S measurement: 
 
• 
The H₂S measuring range is 0–50 ppm, which is relatively low and requires careful sample handling to 
ensure measurement accuracy. Since H₂S is highly soluble in water, the presence of moisture in the 
process gas can significantly affect the measurement. If a gas cooler is used to remove moisture from 
the sample stream, there is a high probability that a portion of the H₂S will dissolve into the condensate 
formed within the cooler. This can result in a reduction of the H₂S concentration reaching the analyzer, 
leading to inaccurate or underestimated measurement results. 
• 
If moisture present in the process gas is not removed before it enters the analyzer, condensation may 
occur on the optical measuring lens of the TDLAS analyzer, potentially affecting measurement 
accuracy and analyzer performance. 
• 
In the event that moisture is present in the process gas, we recommend implementing the necessary 
modifications to the existing sample handling system and sample transport line to ensure reliable and 
accurate H₂S measurement. These modifications have been offered as an optional item under Line 
Item 16 of our quotation. 
 
2. Based on the process conditions & parameters provided, no dust particles are present in the process & Feed 
gas. Hence, we have not considered any Purging / Cleaning mechanism for the H2S Analyzers. 
 
3. We have considered Single Analyzer Panel for 1 No. of Process Gas & 1 No. of Feed Gas H2S analyzer. 
 
4. Since the existing Analyzers installed are for Safe Area, therefore we are offering similar Safe area Analyzers.
