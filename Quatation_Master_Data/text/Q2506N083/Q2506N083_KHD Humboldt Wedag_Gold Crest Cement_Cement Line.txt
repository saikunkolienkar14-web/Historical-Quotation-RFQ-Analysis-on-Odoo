INTRODUCTION: 
It is important to know the exact value of CO, NO & O2 at the kiln inlet to monitor accurately the burning 
conditions. 
CHALLENGES: 
 
Gas analyzer equipment in Cement Plants must be able to withstand a very tough 
environment:- 
 High gas temperature up to 1400 Deg C 
 
 High dust concentration up to 2000 g/m3 
 
 High content of Alkalis, Sulfates and Chlorides 
 
 In addition, high level of mechanical stress and strain 
 
 Usual Complaint of wrong O2 reading due to False Air 
 
 Use of pet coke as fuel (with High Sulphur content) / AlternateFuels 
Resource(AFR’s) 

Conventional gas sampling systems with water cooling have a number of serious 
weak points: 
 
 they block up easily because of the condensation that forms due to the low 
operating temperature 
 
 lime deposits and corrosion with water as coolant 
 
 caking of material on the probe causes excessive mechanical stress 
 
 High cost for maintenance and low reliability

TYPICAL PROBLEMS OBSERVED IN A FEW KILN INLET INSTALLATION 

1. The Probe Filter need to be cleaned once every day. With the clogging of 
the filter the flow was reducing. 
 
2. There is a huge deposition of Materials on the probe which has to be 
poked and removed every two hours. 

3. The Oxygen reading was on the higher side- though there was no 
leakage in the system 
 
4. At the Entry point of the Gas in the probe (port of the probe) was getting 
blocked with dust and as a result flow was reducing. 

5. The Blow back is required every 10 to 15 minutes and reading is not 
available for 1 to 1.5 minutes
