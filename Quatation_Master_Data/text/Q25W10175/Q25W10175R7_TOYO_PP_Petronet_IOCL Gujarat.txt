SECTION 2: ASSUMPTIONS/CLARIFICATIONS 
 
1. All Analyser Vents are connected to common ATM vent header near the shelter through Flame 
arrester & Fastloop return shall be connected to Low pressure flare/Retrun header. For field cabinets 
return tie in/ header to be considered by Adage. 
 
2. FO Cable shall be in customer’s scope of supply. 
 
3. All field Power & Signal cables upto battery limit at shelter/field cabinet end shall be in customer’s 
scope. 
 
4. Serial output for QCM moisture analyser shall be terminated at communication JB at field rack, 
further distribution shall be taken care by customer. 
 
5. All the Makes shall be as per EIL Approved vendor list, Attachment 09B-B862-16-51-VL-9500- 
Master Vendor List Instrumentation. 
 
6. For tag no: 102-AT-11301, MDL for CO is 10ppb. Also it is not possible to get calibration blend for 
CO with 500 ppb. Most likely the CO concentration in the Calibration blend will be much higher than 
its ranges. 
 
7. For tag no: 102-AT-11401, MDL for O2 is 1-2ppmv for GC, Also since normal concentration 
corresponds to LOD value, measurement accuracy shall not be reliable with GC and hence seperate 
3 No of Electochemical O2 analyser shall be quoted along with GC.
