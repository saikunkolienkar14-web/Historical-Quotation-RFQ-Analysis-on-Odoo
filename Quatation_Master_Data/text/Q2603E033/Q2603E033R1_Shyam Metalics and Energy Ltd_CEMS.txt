SECTION 5: AMC SCOPE 
We shall carry out schedule Non-comprehensive AMC for Gas Analyser System for the period of one year. 
 
I. Maintenance of Analyser & Systems shall include the following; 
 
Scope of Preventive Maintenance Services 
M/s Adage Engineers shall carry out the following activities during preventive maintenance visits: 
 
1. Inspection and verification of the proper functioning of all analyser systems and subsystems, including pumps, 
probes, filters, sensors, main analysers, power supplies, and associated equipment. 
2. Preventive maintenance of sampling probes. 
3. Flushing and cleaning of sample lines, leak testing, and verification of sample line temperature, suction levels, 
and overall system performance. 
4. Preventive maintenance of the Sample Handling System (SHS), including filters, internal tubing, and 
associated components. Filter back-purging shall be carried out as per the recommended maintenance 
schedule or earlier, if required. 
5. Inspection for signs of corrosion and implementation of suitable preventive measures. Routine inspections 
shall be conducted to minimize corrosion of probes and other system components. 
6. Verification of the proper functioning of all accessories connected to the analyser system. 
7. Functional testing and calibration of the complete analyser system. 
8. Operation and maintenance of the analyser systems on a regular basis to ensure reliable performance. 
9. Health checks of associated control and monitoring equipment, including PLCs, HMI/PanelView units, and 
other connected devices. 
10. Calibration of analysers using suitable certified standard calibration gases of the appropriate range, wherever 
required. 
11. Verification of the analyser room/environmental conditions and reporting of any deficiencies to the customer 
for necessary corrective action. 
12. Submission of maintenance and functional reports to the designated site-in-charge. Periodic consultations 
shall also be conducted with the designated personnel of the Operations and Instrumentation Departments. 
13. Recording and analysis of maintenance data collected during preventive maintenance visits. The data shall be 
maintained in OEM-recommended maintenance charts to facilitate forecasting of preventive maintenance 
requirements, breakdown maintenance, and component replacement schedules. 
14. All spare parts required for repairs and all consumables necessary for normal operation and maintenance of 
the systems shall be arranged by the Customer. 
15. Working hours shall be as per standard industrial practice, i.e., 8 hours per day.

II. Exclusions to the AMC Agreement 
 
A. Exclusions 
 
The Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) does not include the following: 
• 
Repair of damage resulting from accidents, negligence, misuse, improper operation, absence of voltage 
protection, environmental conditions such as excessive dust, water ingress, storms, or any cause other than 
normal and intended use. 
• 
Services associated with relocation of equipment, addition or removal of accessories, devices, interface cards, 
or other peripheral equipment. 
• 
Electrical, mechanical, civil, or other external work not directly related to the analyser systems covered under 
this agreement. 
• 
Supply of spare parts required for repair or replacement, which shall be in the Customer's scope. 
• 
Supply of calibration gas cylinders, which shall be arranged by the Customer. 
• 
Provision of supporting manpower for housekeeping activities and any mechanical or electrical maintenance 
work outside the scope of the analyser system, which shall be arranged by the Customer. 
 
B. Care of Equipment 
 
The Customer shall provide M/s Adage Engineers with unrestricted access to the equipment covered under this 
agreement to enable effective maintenance services.
