PART A: TECHNICAL SCOPE 

Scope of Work (SOW) 
 
Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) for CV Analyzer 
1. Objective 
The objective of this Scope of Work is to define the services to be provided under the Annual Maintenance 
Contract (AMC) for 36 Nos. of CV Analyzers, ensuring reliable, accurate, and uninterrupted operation 
through preventive maintenance, quarterly full servicing, calibration, and prompt breakdown support. 
2. Equipment Details 
• 
Equipment: CV Analyzer 
• 
Make: Union Instruments GmbH 
• 
Model: CWD2005 
• 
Quantity: 36 Nos. 
• 
Location: JSW Steel Ltd Vijayanagar 
3. Scope of Services 
The vendor shall provide Non-Comprehensive AMC services for all 36 CV Analyzers. 
3.1 Preventive Maintenance (PM) 
• 
Preventive maintenance for all 36 CV Analyzers shall be fully in the scope of the vendor. 
• 
PM activities shall be carried out as per OEM recommendations and best industry practices. 
• 
Preventive maintenance shall include inspection, cleaning, checking of electrical/mechanical parts, 
tightening of connections, performance verification, and replacement of minor consumables if 
required. 
• 
Detailed preventive maintenance activities shall include: 
1. Cleaning of Pre-Purification Unit and refilling of Water & Diesel (Diesel to be provided by 
JSW). 
2. Cleaning of filters, including: 
▪ 
Air Filter of CWD 2005 
▪ 
Fine Filter and Coarse Filter of PURY250E
