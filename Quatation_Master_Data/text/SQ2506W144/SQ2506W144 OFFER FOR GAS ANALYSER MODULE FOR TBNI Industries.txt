BRIEF NOTE TO HIGHLIGHT THE SPECAIL TECHNICAL ADVANTAGE OF OUR ANALYSER 

 
1. The sensor technology in Siemens is much more advanced than the competition. The sensor is a 
patented technology of Siemens called “ Micro Flow sensor “ and are always in the reference 
gas path or embedded in such a way that the sensor is not in direct contact with the 
measurement gas path , thereby making the sensor life unlimited . From the industry feedback 
the sensor life in Siemens Analysers are not less than 20 years where the nearest competition 
has very low sensor life and needs replacement in every 2 to 3 years. 
 
2. The response time of the analysers is very fast and that is the advantage of the Siemens special 
sensors. The T 90 time is 3 seconds vis-à-vis competition where it varies from 3 sec. to 22 seconds 
depending upon the type of the analyzer. 

3. The Siemens analyzer has NO MOVING PARTS. The competition uses a technology in 
 paramagnetic where the sensor has moving part and are very susceptible to vibration, dust 
 ingress and moisture. 
 
4. The sample cells in Siemens Analysers can be opened and cleaned with water. The competition 
 model is extremely difficult to open for cleaning. 
 
5. The,SO2 analyser of Siemens used NDIR technology and also have opto–coupler with 
 compensation feature for moisture cross interference (Please note MOISTURE IS IR SENSITIVE 
 ALSO). This cannot be done in the competition and hence the reading of SO2 will 
 always have interference errors without knowledge of the process people as 100 % moisture 
 removal may not be practically possible to be maintained in practical industry maintenance.

ADVANTAGES OF OPERATION & MAINTAINANCE SPECS 

a) 
Ultramat 23 Analyser from SIEMENS have been extremely rugged and with improved design. The 
sensor is not exposed in the sample gas path and the sensor does not fail due to moisture or dust ingress. 
This is an extremely useful advantage as the sensor life in SIEMENS is much higher. Feedback can be 
taken on this from any SIEMENS analyser user. There are installations for more than 20 years where the 
sensor has not been replaced till date. 

b) 
The SO2 analysis is done through Ultramat 23 analyzer. The Ultramat 23 uses the advanced triple 
layer detector. 
 
Additionally the single beam technology in the Ultramat 23 Analyser allows extremely low drift. The 
conventional dual beam technology used in the CONVENTIONAL models has disadvantages of higher 
influence due to Dirt in sample cell, aging of electronics, reduced IR intensity. Hence frequent calibrations 
with calibration cylinders are required. 
 
In the single beam technology the compensation is much easier;- 
Illustrated as U = Uo X e-ⁿª where the U is detector signal and Uo is base signal with zero gas and na is 
constant multiplied by the concentration of component to be measured . S is the signal generated as a 
ratio of the Uo and U. If F is the influencing factor due to which the detector calibration is required 
frequently , in single beam technology , only F.Uo to be measured frequently by calibration thru the 
non IR gas like ambient air . 
 
Thus till the next calibration cycle this value of F.Uo is used for calculating S, so that the measured value 
F.U gives correct value of S. 
 
Hence the mentioned influence by dirt or moisture or ageing will have only an insignificant effect to the 
measurement as long as the F.Uo is in certain tolerance limit which is monitored thru the Analyser 
electronics. The analyser thus uses ambient air with inbuilt pump for auto calibration. Only once in a year 
or in 18 months calibration is required with a certified cylinder. Therefore the analyzers are virtually 
maintenance free.
