SECTION 1 : ASSUMPTIONS/CLARIFICATION 
 
We have quoted Viscometer by ICON Scientific limited. 
 The following are the clarification: 
a) In the datasheet the Measuring range is provided in Centistroke: 0-1000 cSt, Hence Kinematic 
viscosity (cP) can be calculated directly by the analyser by inputting a density value. 
 
b) The upper limit of the viscosity analyser measurement range is 200 cP. 
c) The Measuring principle of viscosity is by capillary tube measurement method. 
d) Accuracy is not a relevant metric for online physical property analysers and is controlled by the 
quality of on-site analyser calibration; a perfect calibration will provide perfect accuracy. 
e) Repeatability is within the criteria of the ASTM D445 test for the measuring range and type of 
product under test. 
f) HART protocol is not available on the 4-20mA outputs. 
 
g) Power Supply requirement shall be 115VAC 50-60Hz, Max 1000VA.
