The sensor technology in Siemens is much more advanced than the competition. The sensor is a patented technology of

feedback the sensor life in Siemens Analysers are not less than 20 years where the nearest competition has very low sensor 
life and needs replacement in every 2 to 3 years.

moving part and are very susceptible to vibration, dust ingress and moisture.
