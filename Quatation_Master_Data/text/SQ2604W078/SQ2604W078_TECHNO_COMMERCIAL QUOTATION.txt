PART A: TECHNICAL SCOPE 

Scope of Work (SOW) 
 
Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contract (CAMC) for Gas analyser system installed at your site. 
1. Objective 
The objective of this Scope of Work is to define the services to be provided under the Comprehensive 
Annual Maintenance Contract (CAMC) for 2 Nos. of gas Analyzers and system, ensuring reliable, accurate, 
and uninterrupted operation through preventive maintenance, monthly full servicing, calibration, and 
prompt breakdown support. 
2. Equipment Details 
• 
Equipment: Gas Analyzer 
• 
Make: Adage & Emerson 
• 
Quantity: 2 Nos. 
• 
Location: JSW Steel Ltd Dolvi Sinter Plant 2 
3. Scope of Services 
The vendor shall provide Comprehensive AMC services for all 2 nos of gas Analyzers, including sampling 
system. 
3.1 Preventive Maintenance (PM) 
Detailed preventive maintenance activities shall include: 
 
1. Functioning of all system and sub-system of analyser, e.g. pumps, probes, filters, sensors, main 
analyser, power supplies etc. 
 
2. Preventive maintenance of probes. 
 
3. Flushing & cleaning of the sample lines, leak checks, temperature and suction levels etc. 
 
4. Preventive maintenance of Sample Handling System including filters, internal tubes etc. Back 
purging of the filters will be done as recommended in schedule (Whichever is earlier) depending 
upon requirement. 
 
5. To monitor any corrosion and take suitable measures to prevent them. In order to avoid corrosion 
to probes and other parts of the system, it would require routine inspection.
