ADVANTAGES FOR TUNABLE LASER DIODE BASED ANALYZER FOR MEASUREMENT OF HCL 
 
INSITU MEASUREMENT: 
 
You would be aware that measurement of NH3 gas has its own challenges as it is highly soluble in 
moisture and is also corrosive in nature. Conventional Extractive measurement of NH3alwaysremainsa 
question due to high solubility n moisture particularly in low ppm levels. 
 
Hence we have offered the state of the art In situ Laser Gas Analyzer Model LDS6 of SIEMENS AG, 
GERMANY 
for this application which over comes the above challenges as gives the most accurate measurement 
with no cross interference for such low ranges of 100ppmlevel. 
 
MOST ADVANCED SENSOR TECHNOLOGY: 
 
TDLS (Tunable Diode Laser Spectroscopy) which is the most suitable technology for the measurement of 
gases like NH3 
 
SYSTEM DESCRIPTION: 
 
The gas analyzer LDS 6 consists of a central unit which can be connected up to three in-situ sensor pairs. 
The Sensor pairs are mounted cross duct in the stacks. The connection between the central unit and the 
Sensor pair is established by high quality armored industrial grade rugged hybrid cable. A loop cable 
connects the transmitter and receiver parts of the cross-duct sensor. 
 
LOCATION OF LASER SOURCE: 
 
The Laser Source is present in the 19” rack mounted central electronics unit which can be located in clean 
safe location i.e air conditioned control room etc. The laser source is not subject to ambient harsh 
conditions since it is not located in the sensor pair mounted in the stack. This enhances life of the laser 
source. 
 
MULTIPLE CHANNEL MEASUREMENT 
 
Multiple channel versions which measures up to 3 independent measurement paths simultaneously 
 
SIMULTANEOUS REAL TIME READINGS FOR MULTIPLE CHANNELS (AS PER CPCB 
GUIDELINES) 
 
Simultaneous Real Time independent readings are available along with independent 4-20 mA and 
displayed in the central unit for each of the three sensor pairs.
