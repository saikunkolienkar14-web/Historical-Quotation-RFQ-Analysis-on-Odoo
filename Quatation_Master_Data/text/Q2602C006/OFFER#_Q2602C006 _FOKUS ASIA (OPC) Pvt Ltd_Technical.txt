INTRODUCTION: 

 
It is important to know the exact value of SOX, CO, NO & O2 at the kiln inlet to monitor accurately the burning 
conditions. 
CHALLENGES: 
Gas analyzer equipment in Cement Plants must be able to withstand a very tough environment:- 
 High gas temperature up to 1400 Deg C 
 High dust concentration up to 2000 g/m3 
 High content of Alkalis, Sulfates and Chlorides 
 In addition, high level of mechanical stress and strain 
 Usual Complaint of wrong O2 reading due to False Air 
 
 Use of pet coke as fuel (with High Sulphur content) / AlternateFuels Resource(AFR’s) 

Conventional gas sampling systems with water cooling have a number of serious weak points: 
 
 they block up easily because of the condensation that forms due to the low operating temperature 
 
 lime deposits and corrosion with water as coolant 
 caking of material on the probe causes excessive mechanical stress 
 High cost

The Problems & Our Solutions 

 THE ADVANCED KAS PROBE WITH ADDITIONAL FEATURES: 
 
The problems with the gas analysis in cement rotary kilns can be minimized or eliminated by 
using our patented Gas sampling system. 
 
 CAKING OF MATERIAL ON THE PROBE 
Minor deposits are removed during regular automatic retraction by air blasting of 
probe with compressed air. 
 
 HIGH MAINTENANCE COSTS 
Thanks to the high operating temperature and the effective method of cleaning our 
system can achieve periods of maintenance free operation up to three months. 
 
 FAIL SAFE ARRANGEMENT

automatically and saves it from bending due to overheat. 
 
 NEWLY DESIGNED HEAT EXCHANGER

efficiency. 
 
 ADDITIONAL SULFUR ELIMINATION FACILITY
