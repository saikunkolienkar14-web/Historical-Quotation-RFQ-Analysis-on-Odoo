Section 3: General Notes and Clarifications 
 
1. Quoted Prices are in US Dollars $. 
2. Filter is not considered as the its effectiveness against removal of Turbidity and/or Oil cannot be 
guaranteed and purpose of system is to measure Oil and chances of sample contamination due to 
filtration or later release of oil from said filter my lead to sample contamination. 
3. We have not considered Analyzer being installed inside cabinet as Analyzer is suitable for field 
installation, we have considered field skid. 
4. Analyzer body is SS316L and on/Off Switch quoted are Exd type with LM6 
5. Current system design does not allow sample return greater than ambient pressure due to funnel 
arrangement. 
6. We have considered Alloy 825 or better for wetted parts. 
7. Bypass is not considered in the system as analyzer can support upto 25lpm flow. 
8. RF/EMI compliance is covered under CE certification of the Analyzer. 
9. There are no special tools required for operation of the system. 
10. Analyzer have RS485/Ethernet for communication with DCS. 
11. No Type Testing has been considered in current offer. 
12. Service support shall be made available through our facility in Kezad Abu Dhabi. 
13. Following exclusions are not quoted and to be taken care by customer. 
• 
All field tubes (standard tubes, ¼’’ OD SS316, ½’’ OD SS316, ¾’’ OD SS316 etc) 
including sample transportation and return, flare lines, instrument airline up to battery 
limit etc. 
• All field power, signal cables with cable trays and supporting structure as required. 
• Field Cable trays and supporting structures for heat tracer lines. 
• Process isolation valve near tapping point. 
• Any kind of Civil Work or Mechanical Work. 
• 
Erection and Installation of analyser Shelter and hookup of required utilities, Pre- 
fabricated Electrical traced tubes, bare tubes, pipe, tube fittings between 
sample take off and analyzer shelter & sample return, fast loop, vent lines etc. 
• Material Handling at Site, unloading of analysers, Internal Shifting from Plant Store to 
Field. 
• Loop checking for analogue & digital signals related to filed/DCS connection. 
• Providing all utilities as specified in MR at one point/battery limit near each 
Analyzer house / Cabinet / Rack in field.
