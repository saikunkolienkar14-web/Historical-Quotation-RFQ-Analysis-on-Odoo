Section 3: General Notes and Clarifications 
 
1. Quoted Prices are in US Dollars $. 
2. Price Basis Ex Works AKAI- UAE – Excluding VAT and custom duty 
3. Filter is not considered as the its effectiveness against removal of Turbidity and/or Oil cannot be 
guaranteed and purpose of system is to measure Oil and chances of sample contamination due to 
filtration or later release of oil from said filter my lead to sample contamination. 
4. We have not considered Analyzer being installed inside cabinet as Analyzer is suitable for field 
installation, we have considered field skid. 
5. Analyzer body is SS316L however Signal JB and On/Off Sitch quoted are EXd type with LM6. 
6. Current system design does not allow sample return greater than ambient pressure due to funnel 
arrangement. 
7. We have considered Alloy 825 or better for wetted parts, Analyzer shall not be NACE Compliant. 
8. Bypass is not considered in the system as analyzer can support upto 25lpm flow. 
9. RF/EMI compliance is covered under CE certification of the Analyzer. 
10. There are no special tools required for operation of the system. 
11. Analyzer have RS485/Ethernet for communication with DCS. 
12. No Type Testing has been considered in current offer. 
13. Service support shall be made available though our facility in Kezad Abu Dhabi.
