SECTION 1: OFFERING HEADLINES 
 
Below items are in MEIL Scope of Supply:- 
1. Equipment Foundation for mounting of Analyser cabinet assembly. 
2. Standard Power cables for UPS and NON-UPS power supply up to battery limit for lighting up 
the Analyser cabinet. 
3. Standard Signal cables/FO cables from Analyser cabinet mounted JB’s to DCS. 
4. All tubes/piping connections from Analyser shelter Headers to plant flare header/vent. 
5. All the utilities like instrument air, plant N2 up to battery limit. 
6. Installation and Erection of complete System. 
7. Plant Control System DCS/FGS/ESD. 
8. Terminations in junction boxes to connect multipair cable and FO from analyzers cabinets to 
control system. (DCS,ESD or FGS). 
 
Below items are in Adage Scope of Supply:- 
1 Design, Engineering and integration of the complete Analytical system including sample 
probe, sample system, Analyser Cabinet. 
2 4-20mA for each measuring compnent and digital potential free contacts(Alrams/Faults) shall 
be terminated in JB outside analyser cabinet. Further connections to DCS/Control Room shall 
be in MEIL scope. 
3 Redundant FO outputs (through convertor) shall be made avalable upto cabinet mounted LIU 
panels. Further coneectivity shall be in MEIL scope. 
4 The calibration cylinder(with double stage regulator) for 1 year after SAT. 
5 The sample return conditions are not provided. As the Process fluid have SO2, CO, CO2, 
Moisture and N2 gases and the sample is in non Hazardous gas phase same shall be 
returned to ATM vent header/ Flare header.Metal tube rotameters considered for sample 
and fast loop line for all tags. 
6 Analyser Vent is connected to ATM vent through Flame arrester. 
7 We have considered common sample probe and sampling system for CO,CO2 and O2 
analyzer. 
8 The Dew point of flue gas at sample pressure is 65Deg C, and there is 0.25Mol% Sulphur 
with moisture hence we have considered a heated Probe assembly instead of online 
retractable probe to avoid condensation. 
9 We have quoted Vortex cooling arrangement for analyzers cooling. Optionally Ex-Proof Air 
conditioner unit price is also provided.
