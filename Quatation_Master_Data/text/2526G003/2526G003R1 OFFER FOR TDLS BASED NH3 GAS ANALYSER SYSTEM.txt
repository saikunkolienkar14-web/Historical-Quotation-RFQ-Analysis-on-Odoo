SECTION 4: TECHNICAL ADVANTAGES OF GAS ANALYSER LDS 
 
PRINCIPLE OF OPERATION 
 
▪ Simple Insitu Cross type Double Sided based installation with transmitter 
and receiver unit at 180 degree mounting arrangement with connected 
through special loop cable. 

▪ Tunable laser diode is used at transmitter end for producing single 
wavelength beam for measurement inside the process. 

▪ Laser beam gets attenuated due to absorption of gas molecules along the 
path and resultant intensity detected at Receiver end is the proportional to 
concentration of specific gas.
