ADVANTAGES OF OPERATION & MAINTAINANCE SPECS 
 
a) 
Ultramat 23 Analyzer from SIEMENS have been extremely rugged and with improved design. The 
sensor is not exposed in the sample gas path and the sensor does not fail due to moisture or dust ingress. 
This is an extremely useful advantage as the sensor life in SIEMENS is much higher. Feedback can be taken 
on this from any SIEMENS analyser user. There are installations for more than 20 years where the sensor 
has not been replaced till date. 
 
b) The CO analysis is done through Ultramat 23 analyzer. The Ultramat 23 uses the advanced triple layer 
detector. 
 
Additionally the single beam technology in the Ultramat 23 Analyzer allows extremely low drift. The 
conventional dual beam technology used in the CONVENTIONAL models has disadvantages of higher influence 
due to Dirt in sample cell, aging of electronics, reduced IR intensity. Hence frequent calibrations with 
calibration cylinders are required. 
 
In the single beam technology the compensation is much easier;- 
Illustrated as U = Uo X e-ⁿª where the U is detector signal and Uo is base signal with zero gas and na is 
constant multiplied by the concentration of component to be measured . S is the signal generated as a ratio 
of the Uo and U. If F is the influencing factor due to which the detector calibration is required frequently , in 
single beam technology , only F.Uo to be measured frequently by calibration thru the non IR gas like ambient 
air . 
 
Thus till the next calibration cycle this value of F.Uo is used for calculating S, so that the measured value F.U 
gives correct value of S. 
 
Hence the mentioned influence by dirt or moisture or ageing will have only an insignificant effect to the 
measurement as long as the F.Uo is in certain tolerance limit which is monitored thru the Analyser electronics. 
The analyser thus uses ambient air with inbuilt pump for auto calibration. Only once in a year or in 18 months 
calibration is required with a certified cylinder. Therefore the analyzers are virtually maintenance free.
