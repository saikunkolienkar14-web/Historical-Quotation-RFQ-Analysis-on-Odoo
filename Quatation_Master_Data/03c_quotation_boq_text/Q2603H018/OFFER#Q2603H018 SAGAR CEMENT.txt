Features of the offered system.
Analyzer system offered with primary SHS consisting of diesel bubbler, water bubbler and knock out drum
for removal of rugged impurities like tar , naphthalene there by allowing only pure sample to enter into the
analyser there by increasing the reliability of the offered system .
Direct Measurement Principle: Utilizes combustion-based measurement, ensuring accurate determination of
actual calorific value (CV) rather than calculated estimates.
High Accuracy for Variable Gas Composition: Reliable performance even with fluctuating or unknown gas
mixtures such as coke oven gas, blast furnace gas, and syngas.
No Cross Sensitivity: Measures total energy content of the gas, eliminating errors due to individual gas
component interference.
Robust Design for Harsh Conditions: Suitable for dirty and contaminated gases, resulting in reduced
maintenance requirements.
Continuous Real-Time Analysis: Provides fast and continuous CV output for effective process monitoring and
control. Wide Application Range: Capable of handling low and high calorific value gases with optional
support gas arrangement.
Low Maintenance & Operating Cost: Simple design without complex components like gas chromatographs,
leading to lower lifecycle costs.Safe Operation: Enclosed combustion system with no open flame and no gas
emissions ensures high operational safety.
