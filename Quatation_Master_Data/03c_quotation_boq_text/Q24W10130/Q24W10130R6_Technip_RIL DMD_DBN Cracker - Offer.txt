1. We have considered Redundant communication from Analyser shelter as per RPMS requirement
2. Standard power and signal cables upto battery limit of Analyser shelter/cabinet/rack by Technip.
Further internal distribution shall be taken care by Adage.
3. Foundation for mounting of Analyser shelter /Cabinet/Rack and accessories shall be taken care by
Technip
4. All tubes/piping connections from Analyser shelter Headers to plant flare header/drain header shall
be taken care by others
5. Supporting structure, Trays, clamps and other accessories for laying of all signal, power and
communication cables and field tubing’s including EHT bundles shall be taken care by Technip.
6. All drawings for executed project for Reliance JMD site for craker application are enclosed and are
inline with Reliance philosophy. The same standard philosophy will be mainatained for this project
as discussed during F2F meeting.
7. All sample tubes shall be coil tubes.
8. ESD Shutdown valve at sample inlet to be considered for all GC Tags.
9. For Tag No 010-AT-311801(2 stream Moisture measurement), stream switching shall be done in
secondary SHS using SOV using command from DCS.
10. ISJB at shelter to be considered by Adage
11. Calibration Blend in 2 Lot (1 during FAT which will be dispatched after FAT + 1 during SAT).
12. For Trace H2O tags, electropolishing + Dursan coating is considered wetted part MOC of analyser
13. Make of PLC shall be SIEMENS S7 1500 / ROCKWELL
14. As the detail BOM is not provided for each component it would be purely vendor responsibility to
ensure that the system is designed as per RPMS requirement. If during detailed engineering , any
requirement of maintaining sample temp above dew point arises it shall be taken care by Adage
without any impact to RIL.
15. Provision of LP steam sparger to be considered for each shelter and field cabinet
16. For Tag No : AT-310301, AT-311412, AT-311701, AT-312102, AT-312401, AT-312902, AT-314601,
which are GC Tag’s in vapour phase, Sample Bomb collection along with flexible hoses shall be
considered as per RPMS requirement.
17. For Tag No :AT-311805 (Moisture in gas phase), Sample Bomb collection along with flexible hoses
shall be considered as per RPMS requirement.
18. AT-312101 (Moisture), AT-315901 (Ph), AT-316507 (TOC), AT-317006 (PH), in Liquid phase only
sample grab arrangement (with Needle valve) is required and same is considered as per RIL
requirement.

for UPS, Non UPS & Emergency power.
20. For 2 stream GC with parallel injection Separate SHS shall be considered for mounting SHS
components.
