SCOPE OF WORK:
M/S Adage Engineer will check the following during Supervision & Commissioning visit.
1. M/S Adage Representatives will check the loose materials according to BOM.
2. Supervision of field tube laying from analyser panel to scavenging panel & scavenging panel to probe
head for probes.
3. Supervision of field tube connections in the respective junction boxes, properly insulated with insulation
tapes and sealant to prevent it from rain.
4. Supervision of SS tube connections for outer purging of probes.
5. Supervision of all the external wiring connections from field to analyser panel.
6. Leakage test of overall systems.
7. Communication & Logic check of overall systems.
8. Calibration of all the analysers.
9. Commissioning of overall systems.
