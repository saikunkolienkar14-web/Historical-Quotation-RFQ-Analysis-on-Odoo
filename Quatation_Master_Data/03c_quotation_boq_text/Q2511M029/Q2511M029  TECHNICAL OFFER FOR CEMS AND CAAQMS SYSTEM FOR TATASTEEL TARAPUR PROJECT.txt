1. All signal, power & communication cable is in customer scope.
2. Instrument air free from oil, dust & moisture should be made available by customer near
analyser panel & at sampling location for purging for CEMS.
3. Complete stack details for CEMS stack & other SPM stack alongwith 100% sample gas
composition to be provide as per our format.
4. We have considered CEMS analyser system with dust, flow monitor, TT & PT for safe area
application.
5. Porta Cabin considered for mounting CEMS system is also for safe area.
6. O2 measurement is done based on paramagnetic principle with hot extraction technology
along with SO2,NO,CO2 & CO measurement.
7. Moisture measurement is not applicable in our quoted gas analyser system as we are
extracting sample under Hot condition & removing all moisture in condensing unit before
sample gas measurement.
8. Common Shelter is considered for mounting both CEMS & SO2 analyser system.
9. PTZ Camera for CEMS system is in customer scope.
10. Commissioning spare are irrespective of any list & same will be hand carried by our
engineer at time of installation.
