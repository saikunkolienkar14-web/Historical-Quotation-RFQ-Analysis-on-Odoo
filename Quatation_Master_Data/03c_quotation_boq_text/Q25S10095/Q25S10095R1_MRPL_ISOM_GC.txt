Quotation Number: Q25S10095R1
BOQ Status: FOUND

1. All Field power and signal cable shall be in RIL scope of supply.
2. GC & all Electrical equipment shall be rated to Zone 2 llB+H2 T3.
3. Instrument air @5-7 Kg/cm2g shall be provided at existing analyser shelter & at Analyser cabinet.
4. AT 328106 & AT 328107 GC’s shall be mounted inside existing shelter with PDB,
JB’s(ASJB,DSJB,CSJB), headers shall be used existing.
5. 4-20Ma hardwire AO output & Serial output shall be terminated at Analog signal JB &
Communication JB at shelter/cabinet side, further distribution through FO cable shall be in MRPL
