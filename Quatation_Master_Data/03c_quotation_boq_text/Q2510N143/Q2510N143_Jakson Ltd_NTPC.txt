CRCA frame structure. Top inner is of 1.2mm plain sheet.
EXTERNAL & INTERNAL: External sidewalls shall be ribbed
interlocking CRCA sheet of 1.2 mm thickness and internal walls shall
be CRCA sheet of 1.00 mm thickness.
INSULATION: 50mm Rock wool 48kg/m3 Density

W X 2000 H with safety glass window, suitable locking arrangement and
automatic spring door closer opening by simple push.
OTHERS:
1. Hooks for easy lifting and shifting will be provided.
2. Suitable A.C mounting plates will be provided on the side at
required location
3. WITH Door & Cylinder Canopy.
4. Split AC- 1 ton

Porta Cabin shall be treated and painted as follows:
1. All surfaces including structures shall be treated and grinding
done so that they are smooth and free of scale, rust etc.

blemishes on surfaces.
