Note:
1. We have quoted Hot Extractive SO2,O2 System at FGD inlet and SO2,NO,CO, O2 at CEMS where
O2 measurement will be on dry basis & Insitu zirconia O2 at CEMS, where O2 will be measured
on wet basis. So moisture will be calculated with difference in reading from both O2 at CEMS.
2. We will provide counter flange suitable to process connection “4” Flat Faced (FF), ANSI B16.5,
#150” for mounting gas analyser probe, Flow monitor, Temperature & Pressure monitor, SPM
analyser.
3. We have already shared instrument air consumption details.
4. Instrument tubing & fittings will be of Swagelok/parker.
5. Flow, Temperature & Pressure transmitter have isolated 4-20 mA output. Hart o/p is applicable
for Temperature & Pressure transmitter only.
6. Power & signal cable from shelter to sampling point along with cable tray is in customer scope.
7. Drawing 230 VAC supply from Raw 415 VAC supply inside analyser shelter & its distribution at
sampling point for gas analyser probe, SPM, Flow monitor, TT,PT O2 is in our scope.
8. SS tubing from cylinder to panel & from panel to vent is in our scope.
9. Measuring ranges for CO & NO is considered as per CPCB guidelines for FGD application.
10. Temperature transmitter is thermocouple type.
11. Communication cable from shelter to plant DCS for TCP IP communication is in customer scope.
