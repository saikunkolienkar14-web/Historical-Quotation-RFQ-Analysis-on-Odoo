Clarification:
1. We have quoted Hot extractive NDIR technology for NO, N2O, CH4 measurement & UV
technology for NO2 measurement for both CEMS & process application.
2. We have quoted advance Tunable laser diode spectroscopy technology for NH3 measurement
both CEMS & tail gas outlet application.
3. Sample isolation valve for process tail gas inlet & outlet application is in customer scope. We
have considered tapping from pipe for sampling.
4. For tail gas inlet total Nox reading will be calculated from NO & NO2 readings.
5. For CEMS total Nox reading will be on calculated from the reading of NO as per the CPCB
guidelines.
6. All signal, power & communication cable will be in scope of customer.
7. N2/Instrument air free from oil/dust/moisture @4-6 bar pressure shall be provided by customer
near tapping point for both CEMS & Process application.
8. As this export project so calibration cylinder is in customer scope.
9. Data connectivity with local Pollution Control Board is in customer scope. We have considered
data logger in our scope, hardware required for establishing connectivity will be in scope of
customer.
10. Cycle time will be confirmed during detail engineering.
11. Commissioning spares are irrespective of any list & same will be brought by our engineer at the
time of commissioning.
Deviations:
1. For tail gas inlet we have considered NO2 measuring range of 0-27/664 PPM based on the
sample gas composition.
2. Sample gas probe is not applicable for tail gas inlet & outlet process; we will directly take
tapping from line for sample transportation with pressure reduction arrangement at panel.
3. For tail gas inlet SS316 tubing & for tail gas outlet electrically heat trace tube is considered for
sample transportation.
4. Panel MOC considered is CRCA & same will be mounted inside porta cabin with AC, hence panel
AC is not required.
5. PTFE sample tube is considered inside analyser panel.
6. For tail gas outlet NH3 measurement we have considered insitu type advance tunable laser
diode spectroscopy based analyser where sample gas will be taken from line through heat trace
tube & given for analysis to sensor pair mounted on spool piece near analyser panel.
7. Analyser unit will mounted inside tail gas inlet analyser panel, which will be mounted inside
porta cabin so panel AC is not required.
8. Quoted TDLS NH3 analyser is life time calibration free due to inbuilt reference cell. Calibration
gas cylinder is not applicable for same.
9. For CEMS we have considered NO2 measuring range of 0-27/664 PPM based on the sample gas
composition.
10. MOC of sample gas probe is SS 316. Stack ID, OD details for same is required.
11. PTFE heat trace tube is considered for NO,NO2,N2O,CH4,O2 sample gas transportation.
12. Panel MOC considered is CRCA & same will be mounted inside porta cabin with AC, hence panel
AC is not required.
13. MOC of analyser porta cabin considered is CRCA.
