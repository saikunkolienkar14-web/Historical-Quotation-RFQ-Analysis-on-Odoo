& 1102AT0120. We have considered common transmitter for both pH & conductivity sensors.
4. Supply of Probe for analysers & manual sampling system shall be in customer scope.
5. For Tag No: 1101AT0101A & 1101AT0101B, we have quoted Ametek WDG V analyser with a
common HMI.
6. Quoted Analyser (Ultramat 6F) is placed inside an enclosure with vortex cooler and mounted on field
rack. The sampling system is plate mounted and placed on field rack. Please refer to attached
“Annex4_Reference Sullurupeta P&ID & GAD”

4EN.0001”.
8. For manual sampling system: Supply of probe, Sample line/Return line from tapping point to manual
sampling system is in customer scope.
9. For manual sampling system: Supply of Sample cylinder, sample bag, sample bottle is in customer
scope.

(Detail-F), length: 20m

Each mounting plate shall be supplied with sun / rain canopy and suitable for installation on free
standing 2” pipe mounting stanchion.
