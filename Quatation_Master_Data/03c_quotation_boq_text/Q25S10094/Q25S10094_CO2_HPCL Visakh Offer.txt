SECTION 2: ASSUMPTIONS/CLARIFICATIONS
1. We have quoted 2 Nos. CO2 analyser with SHS and a common Analyser cabinet (MOC: CRCA). As
per the data sheet, the analyser panel will mounted inside the analyser room. Therefore, we have not
considered an air conditioning unit in our offer.
2. The distance between the sample take-off point and the analyser panel has not been provided in the
Purchase Requisition (PR). Accordingly, we have considered 30 meters of 1/4” SS316 sample tubing
in our offer.
3. Tubing between the gas cylinder and the analyser panel has not been considered in our scope.
4. All analyser vent and bypass vent lines will be connected up to the analyser panel bulkhead. Any
further connection beyond this point shall be under customer scope.
5. End user shall provide Electrical, UPS, and Non-UPS power supply up to the analyser panel. Further
power distribution within the panel shall be under the scope of Adage.
