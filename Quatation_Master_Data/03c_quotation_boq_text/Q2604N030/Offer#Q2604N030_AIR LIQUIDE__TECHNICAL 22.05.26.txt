NOTES
Liquid Analysers
1. For pH tags Please note that Retractable assembly is not suitable for the high pressure values
provided in the data sheets, hence we have quoted with extractive type configuration with
flowthrough cell and pressure regulator.
2. For tags 08AT 0130003,08AT 0230003 the offered analysers extractive configuration instead of
immersion type as per technical specification due to high pressure limitation .
3. The offered analysers are ATEX with Ex ia IIC T4 certified and will be applicable only for the
transmitters.
4. Please note that for turbidity analyser retractable assembly with wiper is not applicable
Wiper or any cleaning mechanism is required when the range of the turbidity is more than 200
NTU however required range specified in TS is 10 NTU. Secondly, the offered design of turbidity
analyser is such that the sensor within the debubbler mechanism which has constant flow rate
keeping the sensor clean. Also the sensor design is flush and is at an angle of 45 degrees, which
does not allow the impurities to hold on to the sensors for long.
