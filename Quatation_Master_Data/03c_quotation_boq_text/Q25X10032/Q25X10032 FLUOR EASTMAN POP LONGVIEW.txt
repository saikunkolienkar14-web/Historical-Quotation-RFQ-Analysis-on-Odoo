The requirement in the Eastman MR for Analysers and Analyser system is to essentially cover following
activities:
•
Design and Engineering of complete Analytical system including sample probe, sample system,
Analyser Cabinet, AC as per attached specification.
•
However please note that analyzer have been quoted based on assumptions and the entire offer
needs revision when Design Data is made available in form of Datasheets.
•
Analyzer sampling systems consists of basic components that are required for analyzer operation and
are not as per any specifications.
•
Integration of Analyser Systems.
•
Inspection, Testing and FAT as per QA/QC requirement of this project, FAT Loacation Goa India.
Our offer includes all the above-mentioned activities and execution of complete job for this Project will be
done in following manner:
•
Design and Engineering of the complete Analytical system including sample probe, sample system,
etc. will be done at Adage Automation facility in Goa- India. Adage Automation has been authorised
and approved by Valmet for doing the complete design and engineering of sample system / Analyser
Cabinet for the Gas Chromatograph and other Analysers, Adage Automation has already executed
many Analyser System Integration jobs with Valmet Gas Chromatograph and other Analysers for
many Petrochemical and Oil & Gas plants. Also, Adage is approved with all leading Oil & Gas
companies/ Consultants.
•
Application engineering for the Valmet (formerly Siemens) Gas Chromatographs will be done at
Valmet facility in Singapore / Houston. Adage Automation has been representing Siemens (now
Valmet) Gas Chromatograph since last 15 years.
•
Inspection, Testing and FAT shall be carried as under:
o FAT for Valmet Gas Chromatograph at Valmet Houston / Singapore facility (which is same as
Siemens facility as Valmet has taken over same facility and existing resources) no in person
/ remote witness, Test Reports will be reviewed during integrated FAT.
o FAT for other Analyser as per QA/QC requirement at Adage Automation
o Inspection and testing and FAT for Analyser shelter at Adage Automation Goa Factory as per
approved ITP plan.
