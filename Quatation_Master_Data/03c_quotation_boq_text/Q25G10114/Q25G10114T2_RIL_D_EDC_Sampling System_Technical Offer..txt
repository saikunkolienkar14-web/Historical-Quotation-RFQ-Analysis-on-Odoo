Section 2: General Notes / Clarifications
1. Wetted parts
Please note that the we have considered SS 316 as wetted parts for all sampling systems. All components of
sampling system including tubing & fittings are instrumentation components only. For pipe class
RZ109BDRP & RZ309BDRP, we have considered Hastelloy HC276.
2. Units
All valves, tubing & components are in Imperial Units. Pressure gauge unit in kg/cm2g & Temp gauge deg C
3. 3.2 certifications
Since we are using only instrumentation components in our sampling system, 3.2 certs is not available with
none of our sub-vendors. Hence, we will only provide 3.1 Material test certificate as per EN 10204. All
material certificates will be made available for review by customer or customer’s appointed 3rd party
inspector (TPI)
4. End connections
As per section 4.
5. Sample system component size
As per Dopak standard, all components including valves, tubing and fittings (within the sampling system) will
be 1/4". This will avoid dead volume and help in getting more representative samples.
6. Sample Volume
Sample cylinder size is considered 300cc. Sample bottle size is considered 100cc.
7. Cooler
We have considered Dopak standard shell & tube instrumentation type cooler which is more compact and
highly efficient. For all tags requiring cooler, Dopak standard cooler is offered with sample connection 1/4" and
coolant connection 3/8"F NPT, Cooling coil is considered SS 316 for all tags except the Hastelloy tags for pipe
class RZ109BDRP & RZ309BDRP.
8. Components
All pressure gauges for sample pressure reading are direct mounting type. Also, no DBB is considered for
isolation purposes.
9. Documentation
Documents will consist of the following
a. GA drawings, Dimensional drawings & P&ID
b. Hydrostatic Test Procedure
c. PMI procedure & Test results as per Dopak standard
d. Material Test Certificates (EN 10204, Type 3.1)

No further documents considered.
10. NACE requirement
NACE is not considered.
11. Mounting
Sampling system will be SS304 plate mounted on a galvanized steel pipe stand. SS 304 Enclosure is
considered for all tags except Type 33, 34, 40 & 40.1
12. Piping Specification
We use instrumentation components only and hence piping spec & requirements (like CS etc) are not
applicable
13. Surface Preparation and Painting
Painting is applicable only for mounting stand. All other components / enclosure shall be SS finish.
14. PMI
PMI will be done for SS components.
15. Welding & NDE
Not applicable as there is no welding in our systems
16. Start-up & Commissioning Spares Special tools
Not required for our sampling systems.
17. Site Supervision Services
Site services are not applicable as the Dopak sampling systems are plug and play type systems that comes
ready to be installed at site. It’s a purely mechanical system without any electrical components and hence
vendor support is not at all required for the installation and operation.
In case supervision is required then Per diem rate shall be applicable.
