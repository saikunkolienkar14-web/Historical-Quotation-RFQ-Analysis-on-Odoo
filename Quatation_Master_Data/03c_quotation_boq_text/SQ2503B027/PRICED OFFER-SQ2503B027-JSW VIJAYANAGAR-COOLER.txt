SAMPLE GAS COOLER WITH DUAL PERISTALTIC PUMP
Sample gas coolers are used in gas analysis system to cool moisture –
containing sample gas sufficiently so that nowhere in the subsequent
analysis system does it fall below its dew-point.
Presence of moisture in sample gas can falsify the actual reading and
can cause irreparable damage to the Gas Analyser.
While Sampling it is necessary to raise the dew point of the sample
gas, such that the Sample Gas while entering the Sample Gas Cooler
(maintained at a constant temperature lower than the dew point of
the Sample Gas) shed’s all the moisture in the cooler such that the gas
going out of the cooler is a dry gas with constant vapour.

Operation & Construction
In the electric sample gas cooler, the sample gas is cooled to +4.5°C in a heat exchanger. The condensate
precipitated from the sample gas is deposited in the precipitation vessel – precipitator (4) the cooling medium
used is refrigerant R134.A compressor (6) compresses the gaseous refrigerant to approx.7 bar depending on
the operating conditions.

Operating Principle
The Compressor (1) used is hermetically sealed reciprocating type. Downstream of this compressor (1) is a
condenser (2) where refrigerant is liquefied. The heat is dissipated by the means of a Motorized fan (3).
Liquid refrigerant is then collected into strainer (gas dryer) (4). The pressure of the refrigerant is reduced by a
capillary tube (5) for approx.150 psig to 5 psig thus reducing its boiling point. The Low pressure liquid
refrigerant, is then injected into a cooling block(6). Where it absorbs energy from the sample gas from the
heat exchanger inserted inside the cooling block. The vapor refrigerant is then returned back to the
compressor inlet.
The compressor is designed for continuous operation. Once set temperature is achieved inside the cooling
block the SOV operates & hot refrigerant is by passed from the High pressure side to low pressure side thus
reducing the flow of refrigerant to the cooling block. SOV operation is controlled by controller to maintain a
constant temperature in the area of interest.
In case of high temperature a potential free contact is available at the controller so that a High Alarm contact
can be configured.
The electric sample gas cooler is supplied with replaceable heat exchanger with two sample gas circuit (two
cooling systems) However, either one or any one sample gas circuit can be used without disturbing other gas
circuit.
The sample gas line is normally connected with a hose of 4-mm internal diameter 6-mm external diameter. If
screw fitting (bulkhead) are used, then in order to prevent tearing the joints care must be taken to ensure that
the pipe inlets and outlets are not twisted on tightened (e.g. for relieving the strain on the pipe fittings use a
suitable spanner).
The precipitated condensate is, pumped out by peristaltic pumps mounted onto the cooler & operated by in
built controller
