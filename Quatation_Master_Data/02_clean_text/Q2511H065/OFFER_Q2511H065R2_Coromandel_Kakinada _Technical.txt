Measuring component: (based on your requirement)
The siemens laser analyzer model LDS6 has a unique patented feature in which it is a life time
calibrated analyzer. This has been achieved by its unique design of inbuilt calibration cells
through which the laser light is passed, and the laser diode is calibrated by almost 24 times per
second all the time during its operation. This special feature is a patented technology from
siemens and gives the immense benefit of reduction in maintenance costs towards calibration
cylinders and also eases of maintenance.
The LDS 6 is a certified instrument for emission monitoring. The analyzer is TÜV certified as per
EN14181 and also MCERTS of UK.

Key features include:
• in-situ principle, no gas sampling
• three measuring points simultaneously
• temperature up to 1200 °c
• ex-version available (optional)
