System Hardening
The term "hardening" in information security is understood to mean the removal of all software
components and functions that are not absolutely necessary to fulfill a given task. In other words,
hardening summarizes all measures and settings with the goal of
o Reducing the opportunities to exploit vulnerabilities in software
o Minimizing potential methods of attack
o Limiting the tools available for a successful attack
o Minimizing the available rights following a successful attack
o Increasing the probability of detecting a successful attack This is intended to increase
local security and the resilience of a computer to withstand attacks. It follows that a
system can be described as "hardened" if:
o The software components and services installed are limited to those that are required
for the actual operation
