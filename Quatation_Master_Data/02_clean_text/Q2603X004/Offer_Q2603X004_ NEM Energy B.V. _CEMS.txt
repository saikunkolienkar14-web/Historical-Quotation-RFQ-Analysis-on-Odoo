•
Condensate monitor detects moisture and automatically stops the sample pump
•
Prevents water ingress into analyzers, avoiding costly damage and calibration drift
•
Enhances system safety and reliability

•
Timer-based automatic blowback keeps probe free from dust and particulates
•
Ensures consistent sample quality without manual intervention
•
Reduces maintenance effort and improves system uptime

🔹 Heated Sampling Lines
•
Maintains gas temperature above dew point, preventing condensation
•
Ensures no loss of soluble gases (like SO2, NO2)
•
Improves measurement accuracy and repeatability
