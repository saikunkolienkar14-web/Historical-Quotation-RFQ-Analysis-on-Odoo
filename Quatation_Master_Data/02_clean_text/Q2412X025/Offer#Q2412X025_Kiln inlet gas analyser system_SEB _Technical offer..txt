INTRODUCTION:

It is important to know the exact value of CO, NO & O2 at the kiln inlet to monitor accurately the
burning conditions.
CHALLENGES:
Gas analyzer equipment in Cement Plants must be able to withstand a very tough environment:-
 High gas temperature up to 1400 Deg C
 High dust concentration up to 2000 g/m3
 High content of Alkalis, Sulfates and Chlorides
 In addition, high level of mechanical stress and strain
 Usual Complaint of wrong O2 reading due to False Air

 Use of pet coke as fuel (with High Sulphur content) / AlternateFuels
Resource(AFR’s)

Conventional gas sampling systems with water cooling have a number of serious weak points:

 they block up easily because of the condensation that forms due to the low
operating temperature

 lime deposits and corrosion with water as coolant
 caking of material on the probe causes excessive mechanical stress
 High cost for maintenance and low reliabilit

The Problems & Our Solutions

THE ADVANCED KAS PROBE WITH ADDITIONAL FEATURES:

The problems with the gas analysis in cement rotary kilns can be minimized or
eliminated by using our patented Gas sampling system

 CAKING OF MATERIAL ON THE PROBE
Minor deposits are removed during regular automatic retraction
by air blasting of probe with compressed air.
 HIGH MAINTENANCE COSTS
Thanks to the high operating temperature and the effective
method of cleaning our system can achieve periods of
maintenance free operation up to three months
 FAIL SAFE ARRANGEMENT
In case of power or water cooling failure this technology helps to
retract the probe automatically and saves it from bending due to
overheat.
 NEWLY DESIGNED HEAT EXCHANGER
Newly designed heat exchanger comes with bigger coil and fan for high
cooling efficiency.
 ADDITIONAL SULFUR ELIMINATION FACILITY
Separate sulfur removal facility with scrubber filter.
 HIGH COOLING EFFICIENCY OF NEWLY DESIGNED GAS COOLER.
Newly designed gas cooler provides higher cooling efficiency &
moisture removal for better life of Analyser.
