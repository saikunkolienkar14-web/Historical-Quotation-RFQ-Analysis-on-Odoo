General Notes and Clarifications
This covers the offering philosophy and considerations in our detailed offer.

1. Scope of supply is limited to Analyzer, SCS (Primary and secondary).

2. Customer / Contractor supplied and installed tubes shall have provision to be terminated in suitable
Tube OD Connection.

3. Minimum MOC of sample wetted components shall be SS316, PVDF, PTFE and Glass.

4. Area Classification followed for Analyzers are Class 1, Div.2.

5. Tentative Sample Conditioning design is submitted in Annexure-2.

6. We have quoted Mass Spectrometer with capability for measurement of 12 streams.

7. NDIR and Paramagnetic analyzers quoted are standard design and hence OEM FAT is not quoted.
Test Reports for these analyzers will be offered for review during FAT at Adage Works.

8. System response time of 30 seconds cannot be met for all streams with stated sample tube size
and distances. Midrex to revisit requirement of 3/8” Tube and Distances between Tapping points to
SCS for streams.

9. Compliance to 3893-MC-02S is excluded as we are offering KNF Germany Make Sample Gas
Pump and motor shall be as per OEM standard.

10. Mass Spectrometer Functionality test will be done at Extrel Factory.

11. We have considered gas bottles for functionality test for NDIR and Paramagnetic analyzers during
FAT. These gases will be fed through corresponding SCS to Analyzers.

12. VDR have been reviewed and these shall be discussed and mutually agreed during KOM / start of
DDE.

13. Spare Part List will be shared during DDE.

14. Site Services quoted only includes site supervision for commissioning and SAT.

15. All interconnecting tubes, cables between PCS, SCS and Analyzers for Signals, Power Distribution
are excluded from quote.

16. Selection Switch assembly for Sample / Zero and Span Calibration is excluded, in case the same is
to be considered quote shall be revised.
