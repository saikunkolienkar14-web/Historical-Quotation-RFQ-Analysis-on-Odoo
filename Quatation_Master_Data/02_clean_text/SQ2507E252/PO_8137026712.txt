apply on the same.

fully supported by third party invoice i.e. there is one to one correlation between the amount claimed as reimbursement
and the supporting invoice submitted as proof of expense incurred by service provider.

was on UTCL at first place and the expenses was incurred by the contractor on behalf of UTCL merely as a matter of
convenience
