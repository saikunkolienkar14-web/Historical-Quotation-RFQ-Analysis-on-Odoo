oxygen analyser shall be mounted on the field rack.

250 ppb.
6. We have considered Extrel (Process Insights) make Mass Spectrometer.

ULTRAMAT 6F analyser (NDIR) for NH3 measurement.

be cross duct sensor with control unit shall be mounded in purge controlled encloser.

the basis for all analyzers. However, the sample tube lengths for certain tags have not been provided.
Accordingly, we have quoted the tubing on a per-meter basis under optional items. Kindly consider the
required quantities at your end.

proof) air conditioner.
