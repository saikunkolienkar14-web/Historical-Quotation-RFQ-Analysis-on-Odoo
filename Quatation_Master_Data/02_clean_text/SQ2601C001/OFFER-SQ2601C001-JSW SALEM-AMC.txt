1. Purpose & Objectives
Establish a one-year AMC for inlet gas analyzers to ensure safe, accurate, and reliable
measurements, minimize downtime, and maintain compliance.
Primary objective: Supervision of all maintenance activities performed on analyzers
and sample-conditioning equipment.
2. Instruments in Scope
Adage ULTRAMAT and CALOMAT analyzers. Gas components measured: CO, CO2,
O2, H2S. Heated sample line and cooler which are in place and the gas sampling
probe.
3. Scope of Work
Supervision of preventive maintenance, calibration, corrective maintenance,
documentation, and reporting. Includes oversight of sample-conditioning system tasks.
3.1 Preventive Maintenance Supervision (Quarterly & Annual
Intervals)
The Supervisor (vendor’s Lead/Engineer) shall plan, oversee, and verify the following
tasks performed by maintenance technicians (either vendor or customer), ensuring
compliance with OEM manuals and best practices:
ULTRAMAT (NDIR) analyzers

Visual inspection of optics housing, cell contamination, seals, and gas paths;
verify flow/pressure monitoring and alarms per ULTRAMAT features.

supported (ULTRAMAT 23), and confirm drift within acceptable limits; ensure
SIPROM GA or equivalent logs/drift records are captured when applicable.

Verify proper operation of pressure compensation (internal/external sensors)
and measuring-point selection logic where configured (ULTRAMAT 6).
CALOMAT 6 (thermal conductivity)

Inspect sample cell and thermostated sensor integrity; confirm no
condensation/dust per manual guidance; validate measuring ranges and auto
ranging settings.
