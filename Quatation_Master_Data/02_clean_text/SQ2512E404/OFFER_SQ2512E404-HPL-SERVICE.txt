List of Analysers.

intended analyser availability is maintained. Plant shutdown activity shall be planned in advance, HPL
should inform Adage Site supervisor in advance for planning.
3. Periodic servicing and calibration of analyzer to be done as per standard preventive maintenance
schedule defined by OEM standard practices.
4. Each preventive maintenance of the analyzer system includes Servicing, Maintenance & Calibration of
each analyzer system including its sampling system, electronic hardware & software periodic servicing of
sample probes, sample coolers, sample pumps and drain pumps, Adage will change consumable spares
time to time, all the spare parts shall be in HPL scope.
5. Adage will agree to provide the monthly 85-90% availability for all analyser considering to the life cycle of
installed analysers.
6. In case of Emergency or Breakdown maintenance call form site, Adage site engineer will attend within 4
hrs.
7. Adage service Engineer will check of gas cylinder pressure, remove empty cylinder and connect new
cylinder, if required, monitoring the validity of the calibration gas etc. however cylinder refilling and
transportation from site to warehouse shall be in HPL scope of work.
8. Monitoring of Sample handling system, its flow, pressure and analyzer diagnostic alarms etc.
9. Deputed engineer to thrive for reliability improvements steps so that availability and reliability of these
analysers can be enhanced and any unplanned downtime can be avoided.
10. Adage site team will maintain the detailed records for calibration/calibration cylinders, consumables and
other required spares.
11. Service team to perform general shift duties of 8 hours duration (08:00 am to 5:00 pm). During
emergencies, the maximum working hours are limited to 12 Hours. If the person is working during the odd
timing or emergencies or holidays then next working day it will take a leave, same shall be consider as
working day. For emergencies and medical issue, there will be minimum 3 paid holidays on every month
for complete team.
12. Adage will provide all personal protective equipment's like safety helmet, Boiler suit / Apron, safety shoes,
safety goggles and safety belt
13. Adage will provide all basic tools and tackles required to maintain analysers and sample handling system.
