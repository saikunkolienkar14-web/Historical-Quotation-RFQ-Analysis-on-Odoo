SECTION 2: NOTE & SCOPE DEFINITION FOR DATA LOGGER SYSTEM

Scope of Work
•
The Contract scope of work shall include the following as a minimum:
•
Supply and installation of all the IIoT edge devices, firewalls, required splitters & MODBUS converters along with
required cables, conduits etc.
•
Configure all devices and integrate with site analyzers.
•
Implement CPCB-compliant API integration along with requisite encryption/signature.
•
Install required software on MUNPL provided IIOT server hardware.
•
Establish data connectivity to NTPC Servers.
•
Migration of exiting CCTV feed to CPCB through new setup.
•
Set up network segmentation, secure connectivity and configuration of firewalls.
•
All the devices (like edge device, server, firewall) should be compatible with major industry SIEM solutions and
shall be sharing logs with MUNPL on-premises SIEM for monitoring.
•
Provide documentation, training, and support.
MUNPL Scope and responsibilities
•
MUNPL shall providing 230 AC UPS POWER SUPPLY as per requirement.
•
MUNPL shall provision on-prem hardware server for data transfer to Central NTPC Server via API schema
(HTTPS/REST) or MQTT
•
The fetching of data from bidder provided REST API/MQTT broker and transfer to NTPC Aveva PI server shall be
under the scope of NTPC

Scope of Work during CAMC
•
Checking all the hardware systems installed at NTPC Stations for proper data-transmission.
•
Replacing the faulty hardware components (various electronic-modules, including IoT devices , GSM antenna and
cable, power-supply modules, SIM cards, and other accessories) with new components as required.
•
Upgradation of firmware for various electronic modules.
•
Ensuring data-transmission for each panel, through leased-line as well as mobile GSM network.
•
Maintenance-activities of data-transmission Panel/Box like cable-connection tightness, replacing faulty cables
(within the panel, tightness of connectors etc, proper lugs, ferruling and component identification within the panel,
panel-cleaning and proper cable-dressing within the panel, proper panel closure and sealing of gland-holes etc.
•
At the end of each visit the vendor’s maintenance engineer shall prepare a works-report clearly mentioning the
Works-done and Hardware components used during the visit. The report should also mention the availability of
the system during previous quarter.
•
In case of Remote Calibration requirement through MODBUS for any CEMS analyser, the vendor should configure
the edge device as per the requirements (If any hardware required same shall be on NTPC Scope).
Exclusions during CAMC
•
Any damage to the equipment/system due to physical damage, any external source like water /oil ingress,
power fluctuation etc. is excluded from CAMC.

Our Scope of Supply Does Not Include:
•
Any kind of Civil Work or Mechanical Work.
•
Welding of flanges or mounting of purge air unit and instrument on the stack/duct.
•
Material Handling at Site, unloading of Analysers, Internal Shifting from Plant Store to Field.
•
Loop checking for analog & digital signals.
•
Laying of Cable and cable trays for Power supply / Analog and Digital signal / serial communication cables etc.
