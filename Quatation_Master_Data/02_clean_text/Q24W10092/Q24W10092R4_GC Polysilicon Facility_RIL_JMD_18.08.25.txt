SHORT DESCRIPTION OF THE OFFERED SYSTEM

The offered system is based on MAXUM II GCs for both Liquid & Gas applications for complex Polysilicon
process. The sample from the process is tapped and conditioned for any particles in the Primary sampling
system with a self cleaning filter. This Sample is transported to the secondary sampling system fitted with
fastloop redundant filters. The sample from the fastloop is transported back to the process for liquid
samples.
The sample from the analysers are also collected in the gas recovery system & transported back to the
vent header operated at 0.5kg/cm2g.

All the probes, transportation line, Calibration & carrier gases (suitable for 6 months operation after SAT)
with regulators shall be supplied by Adage. Further refilling to be taken care by RIL.

All the GCs and Analysers are having a provision to be connected to DCS through MODBUS TCP/IP for
signal and alarm reporting. These signals are also connected to the Analyser Management System (AMS)
for day to day maintenance.
