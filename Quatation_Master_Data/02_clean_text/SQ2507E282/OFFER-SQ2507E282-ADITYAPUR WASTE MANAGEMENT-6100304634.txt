PART B: TECHNICAL SCOPE

I. Maintenance of Analyser & Systems shall include the following;

M/s Adage Engineer will check the following during priventive maintenance visits.

1. Functioning of all system and sub-system of analyser, e.g. pumps, probes, filters, sensors, main
analyser, power supplies etc.

2. Preventive maintenance of probes.

3. Flushing & cleaning of the sample lines, leak checks, temperature and suction levels etc.

4. Preventive maintenance of Sample Handling System including filters, internal tubes etc. Back purging of
the filters will be done as recommended in schedule (Whichever is earlier) depending upon requirement.

5. To monitor any corrosion and take suitable measures to prevent them. In order to avoid corrosion to
probes and other parts of the system, it would require routine inspection.

6. Functioning of all the accessories connected with the system will be checked.

7. Functional checking and calibration of the total system.

8. The systems shall be operated and maintained on regular basis.

9. Checking the healthiness of such as PLC's, Panel views etc. connected with the system.

10. Calibration of the analysers with the help of suitable standard gases of appropriate range and duly
certified if required.

11. Required calibration cylinder shall be arrnge by M/s West Bengal Waste Management Limited.

12. Verify room environment and report to M/s West Bengal Waste Management Limited for doing the
needful, if needed.

13. Engineer will submit all functional reports to incharge who will consult periodically with Designated
Person of Operating/instrument Department.

14. The maintenance data collected during these regular visits will be suitably plotted in the OEM
maintenance Charts to forecast preventive & breakdown maintenance as well as replacement.
