Section 2: Assumptions/Clarifications

1. Please note that the process data sheets for some of the tags were not available in MR. We have
quoted based on our past experience with similar requirements.
Please be advised that the quoted prices are based on assumptions & are right for quoted models.
2. For Tag No: 401-AT-1003, the specified measurement range is 0–1000 ppb O2. As per received the
email confirmation dtd: 07.04.26, we have quoted Dissolved Oxygen (DO) Analyzer.
3. We understand For Tag No: 401-AT-0202, as per datasheet the requirement calls for H2O & O2
measurement.
4. For Tag No: 401-AT-0203, 401-AT-0601, 401-AT-0602 we have offered a Single GC with 3 stream
sequential measurement and a separate Electrochemical O2 analyser for Oxygen measurement &
H2 analyser.
5. For Tag No. 401-AT-1001, we have considered Amperometric type Conductivity analyzer.
6. Ph, Conductivity, DO sensors shall be of Polycarbonate material and not of Epoxy Coated Die Cast
Aluminium
7. HART o/p is not available for Analyser such as LDTek, Oxygen Analyser.
8. We have assumed the Ambient Temperature conditions to be is 19° C to 45 °C.
9. 24V DC loop powered power supply to be provided by customer.
10. All the quoted prices are in USD.
11. We have considered probe with gate valve for sample isolation. Drawing indicated in the offer.
12. We have considered SS316, electropolished tube, 1⁄4” mm OD x 0.035” thk, for the moisture
measurement analyzer.
13. We have considered SS316, 1⁄2” mm OD x 0.049” thk, for the Liquid Measurement Analyser and
SS316, 1⁄4” mm OD x 0.035” thk for the Gas Measurement Analyser.
14. Sample Conditioning System (SCS) for the field rack shall be mounted on a plate within the rack.
15. The Sample Conditioning System (SCS) for local analyzer cabinets shall be mounted outside the
analyzer cabinet, within a separate enclosure.
16. Network components (Ethernet switches, FO converters, etc.) shall be provided only in the GC
Cabinet for GC connectivity. Other analysers [ moisture, O2 & pH] shall have only 4–20 mA and RS-
485 outputs.
