■Function
Principle of operation
The measuring principle is based on the different thermal con-
ductivity of gases.
The CALOMAT 6 works with a micromechanically produced Si
chip whose measuring membrane is equipped with thin-film re-
sistors.
The resistors are kept at a constant temperature. This requires
an current intensity depending on the thermal conductivity of the
sample gas. This "raw value" is processed further electronically
to calculate the gas concentration.
The sensor is located in a thermostatically-controlled stainless
steel enclosure in order to prevent the influence of changes in
ambient temperature.
To prevent the influence of changes in flow, the sensor is posi-
tioned in a bore located to the side of the main flow.
Note
The sample gases must be fed into the analyzers free of dust.
