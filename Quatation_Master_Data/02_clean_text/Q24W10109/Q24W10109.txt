SECTION 1 : Assumptions / Clarifications

1. No sample probes are considered as the process line sizes are less then 3“.

2. We have considered 10Meters Sample line for each stream.

3. Sample return shall be at low pressure header available at site.

4. Power utilities like UPS and NON UPS power shall be made available by client to GC Cabinet

5. Pneumatic Utilities like instruement Air header, low pressure return header etc. shall be made
available by client to GC cabinet

6. Exhaust from GC shall be connected to vent header through flame arrester assembly.

7. Oxygen measurement of make up gas from OSBL shall be done by Electrochemical oxygen
sensor, which will be mounted in primary SHS assembly.

8. Moisture measurement of make up gas from OSBL shall be done by Al2O3 sensor, which will be
mounted in primary SHS assembly.
