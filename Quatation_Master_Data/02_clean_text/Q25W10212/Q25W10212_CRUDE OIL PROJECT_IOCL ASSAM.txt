NIR MODELING AND CALIBRATION METHOD
Pre-Installation
Introduction

The MOD-4140 System based on principle of Near Infra- Red absorption spectrum measuring. The
system calculates the properties using a calibration model, together with the measured spectrum.
The calibration process consists of several steps:
1. A set of representative samples is collected by the customer.
2. The desired properties (reference data) are measured in the customer’s laboratory.
3. The sample spectra are measured by MOD-4140 installed at the plant.
4. The spectra and reference data are transferred to the Modcon Systems Ltd Service Center.
5. Calibration models are prepared at the Service Center.
6. The calibration models are transferred to the customers system via the internet.

Pre - Installation Calibration

Samples
Sample spectra will be measured by on-line MOD-4140 that is installed at the customer’s site. The
laboratory should provide Modcon with its usual printout of lab results for the samples.
The samples should include the normal operating range of the process unit, to ensure that the
calibration model will cover the full range of conditions. The exact number of samples required will
depend on the particular application.

Reference data
The reference data is generated in the customer's own laboratory, or in an outside laboratory.
Usually these will b e routine measurements that the laboratory performs in its day-to-day operations.

Models
Transfer of the spectra and reference data to Modcon will be done by internet connection.

Calibration Updates
During the first few weeks of operation it may be necessary to further improve the calibration models.
The original set of samples used to create the model may not have covered the full range of operating
conditions, or the operating conditions themselves may have changed over time.
If the operating conditions change, the models may need to be upgraded, even after many months of

system, utilizing the available plant data for automatic model tuning. Utilization factor of the analyzer is
