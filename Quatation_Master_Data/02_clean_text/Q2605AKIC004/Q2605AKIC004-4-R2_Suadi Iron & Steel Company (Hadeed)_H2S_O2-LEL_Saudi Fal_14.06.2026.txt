General Notes:

1. Based on the process conditions and parameters provided, we have assumed that the process/feed gas is free
of moisture and dust. However, as stated in our quotation, the process gas must be moisture-free to ensure
accurate H2S measurement.

There are several factors that need to be considered with respect to H2S measurement:

•
The H2S measuring range is 0–50 ppm, which is relatively low and requires careful sample handling to
ensure measurement accuracy. Since H2S is highly soluble in water, the presence of moisture in the
process gas can significantly affect the measurement. If a standard gas cooler is used to remove
moisture from the sample stream, there is a high probability that a portion of the H2S will dissolve into
the condensate formed within the cooler. This can result in a reduction of the H2S concentration
reaching the analyzer, leading to inaccurate or underestimated measurement results.
•
If moisture present in the process gas is not removed before it enters the analyzer, condensation may
occur on the optical measuring lens of the TDLAS analyzer, potentially affecting measurement
accuracy and analyzer performance.
•
In the event that moisture is present in the process gas, we recommend implementing the necessary
modifications in the sample handling system to ensure reliable and accurate H2S measurement. These
modifications have been offered as an optional item under Line Item 17 of our quotation.

2. Based on the process conditions & parameters provided, no dust particles are present in the process & Feed
gas. Hence, we have not considered any Purging / Cleaning mechanism for the H2S Analyzers.

3. We have considered Single Analyzer Panel for 1 No. of Process Gas & 1 No. of Feed Gas H2S analyzer.

4. Since the existing Analyzers installed are for Safe Area, therefore we are offering similar Safe area Analyzers.

5. The stream compositions provided do not add up to 100%, currently the total composition coming out to be
95+% and balance certain part is unknown. Our concern is that unknown components may interfere in
measurement. If that happens there can be possibilities of change of the offering.

6. Any requirements arising from further discussions that are outside the scope of this quotation shall be charged
separately and quoted accordingly at a later date.
