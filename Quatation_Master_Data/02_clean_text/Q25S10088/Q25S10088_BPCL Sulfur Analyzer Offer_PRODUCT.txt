Short Description of the offered System

The offered System is based on Pulsed UV Fluorescence Detection (Oxidative Pyrolysis) for measurement
of Sulphur in Liquid (Diesel Product).
The Make of the analyzer is PAC & Model is N-Sure. The system has been conceptualised based on the
Datasheet provided to us as per the tender documents and issued corrigendums.
Sample will be tappped from the sample tapping maintained at suitable temp and transported to the
analyzer through a Sample handling system fitted with Fast loop redundant filter (0.5 micron ).
The Calibration philosophy of the analyzer is based on Manual sample filling into the vessel through a
pneumatic pump fitted within the system. The sample from the fastloop and the analyser is connected to a
redundant pump based sample recovery system connected back to the process.
The analyser will be calibrated with 0-20 ppmw range (with all the outputs considered as per the datasheets
requirements.)
