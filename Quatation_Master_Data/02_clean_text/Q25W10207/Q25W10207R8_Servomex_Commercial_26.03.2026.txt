APPENDIX-A

1. CONSTRUCTION OF ANALYSER SHELTERS :
A) THE EXTERNAL WALL OF THE SHELTER SHALL BE 2 MM THICK.
B) THE INNER WALL PANEL OF ANALYSER SHELTERS SHALL BE FABRICATED FROM 1.5MM THICK, SS316 STAINLESS STEEL SHEET. THE INNER WALL SS316 SHEETS MAY BE RIVETED IN PLACE, ,
WITH SS RIVETS, IF THEY ARE SUITABLY SEALED AGAINST THE FRAMEWORK.
C) EACH ANALYSER SHELTER SHALL HAVE MINERAL FIBER INSULATION SANDWICHED BETWEEN THE INNER AND OUTER WALLS. THE INSULATION SHALL HAVE A MINIMUM THICKNESS OF 50MM.
D) EACH ANALYSER SHELTER SHALL HAVE A FREE VENTING TYPE MINIMUM 1000MM CANOPY ALONG EACH SIDE TO PROTECT EXTERNAL EQUIPMENT. THE CANOPY SHALL BE PROVIDED IN
ALL THE FOUR CORNERS ALSO.
E) ALL MATING JOINTS SHALL BE FULLY WELDED / RIBBED INTERLOCKED AND FIXED BY WELDING TO A MAIN SUPPORT FRAME AND EACH SECTION TO HAVE GUSSETS WELDED IN POSITION.
2. ANALYSER SHELTER FLOOR:
A) USE FULLY WELDED DURBAR FLOOR PLATES OR EQUAL, ALUMINUM CHEQUERED PLATES.
B) THE FLOOR SHALL BE COATED WITH A DURABLE ANTI-SKID, ACID RESISTANT EPOXY COATING ON WEAR SURFACES.
C) MUST ENSURE THAT NO GAPS ARE LEFT BETWEEN THE CHEQUERED PLATE AND THE SHELTER WALL.
D) THE FLOOR OF THE ANALYSER SHELTER SHALL SLOPE TO THE DOOR(S).
E) PROPER DRAIN ARRANGEMENT WITH MESH WELL BE PROVIDED TOWARDS THE FLOOR SLOPE. A DRAIN PIPE SHALL BE BROUGHT OUT OF THE SHELTER ON THE SIDES AND PLUGGED SUITABLY.
3. ANALYSER SHELTER DOORS:
A) EACH ANALYSER SHELTER SHALL HAVE 2 DOORS. 1 MAIN ENTRY DOOR AND 1 EMERGENCY EXIT DOOR.
B) THE POSITION AND SIZE OF DOORS AND THE ACCESS TO THEM SHOULD BE ARRANGED TO PERMIT REMOVAL OF EQUIPMENT SHELTERED.
C) MINIMUM SIZE OF DOOR SHALL BE 900MM WIDE BY 2100MM HIGH.
D) THE BOTTOM OF THE WINDOW SHALL BE 1.4 METER FROM THE CONCRETE LEVEL AND SHALL BE 300MM X 300 MM IN SIZE.
E) THE DOOR SHALL BE OPENING TOWARDS RIGHT I.E. DOOR HINGES SHALL BE ON THE RIGHTHAND SIDE OF THE DOOR.
F) EACH DOOR SHALL BE FITTED WITH A PANIC BAR WHICH SHALL OVERRIDE THE DOOR LOCK AND SHALL BE EQUIPPED WITH SELF-CLOSING SPRING SYSTEM.
G) BOTH THE DOORS SHALL BE PROVIDED WITH DOUBLE GLAZED VIEWING WINDOWS.
H) DOORS SHALL BE FITTED WITH SWITCHES WHICH SHALL INITIATE AN ALARM IF OPEN FOR LONGER THAN 120 SECONDS.
4. ANALYSER SHELTER ROOF:
A) ANALYSER SHELTER ROOFS SHALL BE CONSTRUCTED OF THE SAME MATERIAL AS THE MAIN SHELTER (STAINLESS STEEL) AND BE INSULATED TO R19 AT A MINIMUM.
B) SHELTER ROOF SHALL HAVE A CENTER PITCH FOR RAINWATER DRAINAGE, COMPLETE WITH GUTTERS AND DOWNPIPES AND DESIGNED TO WITHSTAND UNIFORM LOADINGS AT 0.75 KN / M2.
C) IT IS CAPABLE OF HANDLING LOAD OF AT LEAST 4 PERSONS WITHOUT ANY PERMANENT DEFORMATION.
D) THE ROOF SHALL HAVE ADEQUATE SLOPE TO PREVENT ANY WATER LOGGING.
5. LIFTING:
A) LIFTING OF EACH ANALYSER SHELTER SHALL BE BY SPECIFIED “EYE-BOLTS”, MOUNTED ON EACH ANALYSER SHELTER ROOF.
B) THESE EYE BOLTS SHALL BE SIZED TO TAKE LOAD OF ANALYSER SHELTER FULLY FITTED WITH EQUIPMENT.
6. AIR CONDITIONING AND VENTILATION SYSTEM:
A) EACH ANALYSER SHELTER SHALL BE AIR CONDITIONED BY TWO PACKAGE AIR HANDLING UNITS (HVAC) WHICH SHALL BE CAPABLE OF PROVIDING REQUIRED VENTILATION AIR SUPPLY
THE HVAC PACKAGE SHALL BE MOUNTED ON THE SHELTER PLINTH AREA WITH SUITABLE ANTI VIBRATION PAD.
B) THE ANALYSER SHELTER SHALL SUPPLY THE COMPLETE REDUNDANT AIR CONDITIONING AND VENTILATION SYSTEM INCLUDING A VENTILATION STACK WHICH SHALL BE
LOCATED ON THE TOP OF EACH ANALYSER SHELTER. THE SYSTEM SHALL BE CAPABLE OF MAINTAINING THE ANALYSER SHELTER AT THE FOLLOWING CONDITIONS: TEMPERATURE
22°C + / -2°C HUMIDITY 50% RH + / -5%.
C) EACH HVAC UNIT SHALL COMPRISE OF FAN SECTION, PRE-FILTER, DIRECT EXPANSION COOLING COIL AND REFRIGERATION COMPRESSOR AND ASSOCIATED AIR-COOLED CONDENSE.
D) HVAC WITH ALL ITS ASSOCIATED EQUIPMENT, INCLUDING ANY CONTROLS, SHALL BE SUITABLE FOR USE IN A ZONE 1 AREA.
E) HE LOUVERS SHALL BE PROVIDED WITH BIRD SCREENS.
F) THE ANALYSER SHELTER SUPPLIER SHALL ENSURE AT LEAST 12 AIR VOLUME CHANGES PER HOUR.
G) EACH ANALYSER SHELTER SHALL HAVE AN AIR INTAKE VIA A COMMON STACK (FOR BOTH HVAC UNITS), MOUNTED ON THE ANALYSER SHELTER ROOF. THESE STACKS SHALL BE
PROVIDED WITH A RAIN HOOD AND A MESH TO PREVENT ENTRY OF BIRDS ETC. MINIMUM 6MM THICK, SS GUY-WIRES SHALL BE PROVIDED FOR SUPPORT OF STACK.
H) THE FRESH AIR INTAKE STACK SHALL HAVE FILTERS 5 MICRONS DOWN TO 99% EFFICIENCY, FIRE DAMPERS AND LOUVERS.
