Section 2: Assumptions/Clarifications

1. The End User is KMG Petrochem & the site location is in Kazakhstan.
2. Ambient condition is -36° C to 44 °C.
3. All the quoted prices are in USD.
4. We have considered probe with gate valve for sample isolation. Kindly confirm in-case other type of
valve is required due to winterization.
5. We have considered an insulated SS316 tube, 6 mm OD, where the sample dew point is −74 °C
after the PSCS.
6. We have considered an electrically heated SS316 tube, 6 mm OD, where the sample dew point is
close to the minimum ambient temperature after the PSCS.
7. We have considered an electrically heated SS316, electropolished tube, 6 mm OD, for the moisture
measurement analyzer.
8. We have considered an electrically heated SS316, Sulfinert-treated tube, 6 mm OD, for the sulfur
measurement analyzer.
9. We have considered isolation valve (air operated valve) in sampling system operated by Toxic gas
detector when there any leak in the shelter as well as in the cabinet.
10. The Analyser House contains 3 compartments/Sections. One is for Analyser room. Second is for
SCS and Third is for cylinder rack.
11. HVAC is Single unit with redundant fan configuration.
12. SMART sampling system is considered only for GC sampling system. Including Pressure
transmitter, temperature transmitter & Differential pressure switch for filter (Bypass filter) switchover.
13. Gas Chromatograph shall be free issued by Valmet.
14. Sample conditioning system for local cabinets shall be mounted inside the analyser cabinet.
15. Network components (Ethernet switches, FO converters, etc.) shall be provided only in the GC
shelter for GC connectivity. Other analysers [ moisture, O2 & pH] shall have only 4–20 mA and RS-
485 outputs.
16. pH Analyser & SCS shall be mounted inside the SS316heated enclosure placed on field rack.
