SECTION 2- NOTE & SCOPE DEFINITION FOR GAS ANALYSIS SYSTEM

• All Interconnecting Communication Cables/ Signal Cable & Powe Cable & Communication Hardware Between
Analyzers to Sample Point Location Shall Be in Customers Scope.
• All return to process/drain connections shall be taken care by customer
• Plant Instrument air & Nitrogen required
• Internet connectivity @ 1 MB/s speed with static public IP shall be provided by customer for data connectivity.
• Analyzer drain line shall be connected to the plant nearest drain, the tubing for the same shall be in
Customer’s scope.
• Power cable, Signal cable, OFC Cable & Splicing of OFC, SS tubing from panel to cylinder and panel to vent
out is in scope of customer.
• All internal panel tubing and fitting will be PTFE

Power Supply Utilities required are:

• Power Supply: 230 VAC, 50 Hz (NON UPS)
• Power Supply: 230 VAC, 50 Hz (UPS)

Our Scope of Supply and Work Includes:

• Design, Engineering and Supply of complete Analyzer System as per Summary of Prices
• Supervision of Erection/Installation and Commissioning of the complete System
• Project Management and Project Documentation

Our Scope of Supply Does Not Include:

• All Erection Activity.
• Any kind of Civil Work or Mechanical Work.
• Material Handling at Site, Loading/unloading of Analyzers, Internal Shifting from Plant Store to Field
• Loop checking for analog & digital signals. (Adage shall provide assistance)
• Laying of cables/cable trays/structural for field tubing.
• All utilities as mentioned above.
• M/s Adage shall provide all the installation guidelines for the system after the detailed Engineering.
