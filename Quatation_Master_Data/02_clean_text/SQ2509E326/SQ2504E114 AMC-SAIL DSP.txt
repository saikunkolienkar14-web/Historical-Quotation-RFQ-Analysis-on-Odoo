TECHNICAL SCOPE

Work: Annual Maintenance Contract for 1 No. of CV analyser.

1. SCOPE OF WORK:

Nature of activities: Preventive Maintenance, Servicing & Corrective Maintenance of Complete 01 No of
M/s Union Instruments, GmbH Make CV Analyzers installed at SAIL DSP. During the entire period of
contract the service engineer shall make 4 normal visit and 2 emergency visit, each lasting for two days and
one day shall mean eight working hours.

a) Schedule of regular visits:
Adage Automation Pvt Limited engineer will make one visit in every three months for the said activities.
The day and time of visit shall be intimated by DSP about two weeks before the visit. Each visit will be for
two days. (Working hours shall be 8 hours/day). At the end of visit, a service report shall be jointly
prepared by the Adage Automation Pvt Limited Service engineer and a representative of DSP. During
working, DSP Engineers and technicians shall witness and facilitate the work being done by Adage
Automation Pvt Limited. This AMC includes maintenance of one no of analyser.

Following activities to be carried out by Adage Automation Pvt Limited in each regular visit:

1. Checking of all system and sub-system of analyzer, e.g. pumps, probes, filters, sensors, main analyzer,
power supplies etc.
2. Preventive maintenance of probes.
3. Flushing & cleaning of the sample lines, leak checks, temperature and suction levels etc.
4. Preventive maintenance of Sample Handling System including filters, internal tubes etc. Back purging of
the filters has to be done as recommended in schedule (Whichever is earlier) depending upon
requirement.
5. To monitor any corrosion and take suitable measures to prevent them.
6. Functioning of all the accessories connected with the system to be checked.
7. Functional checking and calibration of the total system.
8. The systems have to be operated and maintained on regular basis.
9. Checking the healthiness of PLC's, Panel views etc. connected with the system.
10. Calibration of the analyzers with the help of suitable standard gases of appropriate range and duly
certified.
11. Verify room environment and report to M/s DURGAPUR STEEL PLANT for doing the needful.
12. Engineer has to submit all reports to in-charge.
13. The maintenance data collected during these regular visits has to be suitably plotted in the
