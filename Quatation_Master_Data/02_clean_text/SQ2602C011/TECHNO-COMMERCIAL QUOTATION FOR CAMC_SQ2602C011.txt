Name of Work: Comprehensive AMC for M/s Siemens make Stack Ammonia analyzers for A Stream & B

1. The Comprehensive Annual Maintenance Contract (CAMC) is envisaged for maintenance of Siemens
make Stack Ammonia Analyzers for NP Plant A & B streams at FACT-CD.

2. The Period of contract shall be Three years from the date of work-to-proceed notice.

3. Planned regular visit: This CAMC includes 06 planned regular visits on half yearly basis. Planned visit
should be intimated 7 days before visit.

4. Emergency Visit: In the event of breakdown of the Analyzer system, upon a telephone call / email, M/s
Adage deputed Engineer shall reach at FACT site at the earliest, to attend the problems and restore the
Analyzer system back to normal state within 72 working Hrs.

5. Emergency situation is defined as -
i) Failure in the Analyzer system, such as not responding to sample & Calibration gas, Analyzer display
hanging, Modbus communication problem, No uploading of data / error value uploading from Analyzer
to CPCB system etc.
ii) Failure of Analyzer system`s internal power source.

6. All works to be carried out with prior approval and presence of Engineer-in-charge of M/s FACT. After
completion of the work, a detailed report covering the work executed, shall be submitted by M/s AAPL. An
MOM shall be prepared after completion of each visit or service and shall be duly signed by M/s FACT and
M/s AAPL representatives.

7. Ammonia analyzer reference validation (if required) shall be arranged by contractor & validation
certificate to be provided.

8. Reference validation kits shall be validated at M/s Adage factory / HO (if required) and same shall be
arranged by contractor & validation certificate to be provided during the contract period.
