SCOPE OF WORK AND SPECIAL TERMS & CONDITION

1. Name of Contract: Annual Maintenance Contract (AMC) for Ametek make Analyzers of NRL
2. The AMC includes the following analyzers:
a) O2 Analyzers – 11 Nos (Model : WDG-IV & WDG-V)
b) Feed Gas/H2S Analyzer – 5 Nos (Model : 931S & 931)
c) Tail Gas H2S/SO2 Analyzer– 1 No (Model : 880NSL)
d) Any other Ametek make Analysers
3. Contractual period: 5 Years from the date of first visit.
4. Payment Terms: Pro rata basis against the submitted invoice after the completion of each visit.
5. No. of Visits: There shall be two types of visits-
a) Preventive Maintenance
b) Emergency Maintenance.
During the AMC period no. of site visits shall be 3 (three) each year, i.e. once in 4 months. Each PM Visit shall be of
minimum 8 days and extendable for 1 more day as per requirement at site. Duration of SOS visit shall be as per
actual requirement at site for rectification of the equipment and return back to normal operating condition.
6. Scope of Services:
a) Preventive Maintenance
b) Emergency Maintenance

a) Preventive maintenance:
Preventive maintenance service shall be provided at a scheduled interval of four months. Date of visits to be
as discussed and mutually agreed. It shall include inspection of general healthiness of the system, study and
advice on daily maintenance, if any problem is reported, and running of test programs, on-line servicing and
solving reported problems.

Details of services under preventive maintenance are as follows:
i. Checking the general operation & operating condition of the equipment.
ii. Calibration of the equipment and submission of calibration report.
iii. General healthiness checks of various important components and cards: Service Engineer will diagnose for
general healthiness of the equipment using his service tools according to servicing procedure.
- Servicing and Cleaning of Analyzer Sample Handling System.
- Checking of Power Supplies & System Earthing.
- Checking of Hardware components (HMI, Controller, Probe, Heat trace line, Sample cell, Cadmium lamps,
various electronic boards etc.) as well as software of the analyzers.
- Checking of Vortex cooler for cooling inside cabinets.
- Creation of a backup file for emergency purposes.
iv. Investigation of the problems reported by NRL: The problems highlighted by NRL during the visit and hidden
problems identified by Service engineer to be investigated. Vendor engineer will take appropriate / remedial
steps for resolving these problems.
v. Replacement of defective parts: As per requirement if any part is to be replaced, same shall be replaced with
subject to availability of the part at NRL stock.
vi. After completion of servicing, service engineer will demonstrate the performance of the equipment to the
satisfaction of NRL.
vii. Vendor shall maintain the existing operating and application software for any debugging requirements to have
consistent performance of the system.
