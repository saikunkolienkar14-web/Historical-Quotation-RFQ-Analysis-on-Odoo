Thank you for your interest in the analytical solutions from Adage Kanoo Analytical Industry LLC.
Following our recent discussions, please find attached our proposal for the Gas Chromatograph (GC) system,
complete with a 3-sided shelter designed specifically for field mounting.
I would like to highlight a few key technical aspects of our offer:
Low Sample Pressure (Tags 21-AT-001 & 31-AT-001): Due to the low pressure at the Main and HC
Flare Knockouts, we have included a sample booster as part of the sampling system. This secondary system
will be conveniently mounted inside the 3-sided shelter.
Variable Process Conditions (Tags 20-AT-522 & 20-AT-514XX): To ensure the GC accurately handles
both normal operations and plant startup conditions, we have configured two methods within the GC
application.
As per the TQ response, we have excluded the H2S, CO, CO2 measurement for all TAGS.
For Tag# 20-AT-522 (Hydrogen Rich Fuel Gas) – We have configured GC to measure N2 & O2 separately. This is
to ensure that the measured LHV is accurately.
Application Details: Please refer to the application note on the following page for more specifics regarding the
proposed Flare and Fuel Gas analysis.
